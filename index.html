In-vivo MRI of noradrenergic and dopaminergic nuclei predicts future cognition and Alzheimers disease progression🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-02 · Zhao, W., Guzman, S. J., Yoo, H. J. … Nation, D. A., Dahl, M. J., Mather, M.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Noradrenergic and dopaminergic nuclei modulate cognition, and post-mortem data indicate they are affected early in the neurodegenerative cascade of Alzheimers disease. We derived in-vivo locus coeruleus (LC) and substantia nigra-ventral tegmental area (SN-VTA) MRI integrity measures from 739 ADNI participants, examined MRI-histology correspondence in individuals with both in-vivo imaging and post-mortem data, and tested whether these measures reflect early MRI-indexed degeneration predicting later cognitive decline. LC and SN-VTA contrast predicted cognitive and behavioral function 10 years later, despite only weak associations with current function. LC- and SN-VTA-associated brain-wide atrophy patterns aligned with PET-derived norepinephrine-transporter and dopamine-receptor maps, respectively. A one-standard-deviation decrease in baseline LC contrast was associated with 84% higher five-year odds of MCI-to-AD progression, and predictive effects of the catecholaminergic nuclei were mediated by their corresponding atrophy patterns. The SN-VTA pattern was associated with less amyloid PET-related memory decline. Together, these findings establish LC and SN-VTA MRI contrast as markers of early neurodegeneration that forecast cognitive decline years before clinical onset.

Phenotypic screening in Parkinson's iPSC-dopamine neurons identifies the FASN-SREBP1 axis as a potential therapeutic target🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-01 · Caiazza, M. C., Brandes, J., Barrow, E. R. … Smith, D., Ryan, B., Wade-Martins, R.（共 18 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Parkinson's disease (PD) remains highly challenging to model in vitro, particularly when seeking robust functional phenotypes for target discovery. Patient-derived induced pluripotent stem cell dopaminergic neurons (iPSC-DANs) offer a promising platform to address this challenge. Because calcium dyshomeostasis is well-described in PD pathophysiology, it offers a platform for phenotypic screening. We have previously shown that GBA-N370S iPSC-DANs display impaired intracellular calcium release. Here, we used this disease-relevant phenotype to undertake a 4,108-compound phenotypic screen in GBA-N370S (GBA-NS) iPSC-DANs. This screen identified fatty acid synthase (FASN) as the most enriched protein target. The ability of compounds to rescue calcium release correlated with biochemical potency against FASN across multiple chemical series. We confirmed that FASN knockdown increased intracellular calcium release, supporting an on-target effect. Untargeted lipidomics demonstrated broad remodelling of the neuronal lipidome after FASN inhibition, confirming target engagement. FASN inhibition also increased active a-glucocerebrosidase (GCase), reduced extracellular a-synuclein release and ER stress, and altered RASGRF1 expression. Mechanistically, FASN inhibition activated sterol regulatory element-binding protein 1 (SREBP1), and SREBP1 overexpression alone was sufficient to increase intracellular calcium release, identifying a FASN-SREBP1-calcium homeostasis axis in human neurons. Finally, the FASN inhibitor BI 99179 achieved brain exposure and reduced brain fatty acid levels while inducing an SRE-associated transcriptional response in a Parkinson's mouse model, providing evidence of in vivo target engagement. These findings establish patient iPSC-neuron functional screening as a tractable discovery platform to identify targets which can be taken in vivo and nominate the FASN-SREBP1 axis as a candidate therapeutic pathway in PD.

Molecular MRI of Neuroinflammation in Alzheimer's Disease using a Folate-targeted Nanoprobe🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-01 · Badachhape, A. A., Bhandari, P., Khan, R. … Annapragada, A. V., Ritzel, R. M., Ghaghada, K. B.（共 13 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Background: Neuroinflammation, driven by activated microglia, plays a central role in the pathogenesis of Alzheimer's disease (AD). Non-invasive imaging of neuroinflammation holds promise for advancing our understanding of disease mechanisms and evaluating anti-inflammatory therapeutic strategies. The folate receptor (FR), specifically its {beta} isoform, has been identified as a marker of pro-inflammatory myeloid cells in peripheral inflammatory diseases; however, its association with AD neuroinflammation remains unknown. In this work, we first determined FR expression in microglia in mouse models of AD and subsequently evaluated molecular MRI (mMRI) using a folate-targeted nanoprobe for in vivo imaging of the folate receptor expression. Methods and results: Microglial activation and FR expression in APP/PS1 and P301S transgenic mouse models of AD was measured using flow cytometry. A FR-targeted nanoprobe for mMRI was prepared by functionalizing high T1-relaxivity liposomal gadolinium nanoparticle with folic acid. Following intravenous injection, localization of the nanoprobe in the brains of aged transgenic and wild-type mice was measured using T1-weighted MRI. Post-mortem microscopy measured nanoprobe localization relative to microglia and amyloid pathology. In both mouse models, FR{beta} was significantly (p < 0.05) upregulated in pro-inflammatory microglia expressing TNF and IL-1{beta}. The folate-targeted nanoprobe demonstrated MR signal enhancement in the cortical and hippocampal regions of transgenic mice but not in wild type mice; non-targeted nanoprobe control did not demonstrate signal enhancement in brain of transgenic mice. Microscopy confirmed colocalization of FR-targeted nanoprobe with IBA1-positive microglia/macrophages and amyloid plaques. Conclusions: Pro-inflammatory microglia exhibit elevated levels of FR, and its measurement by non-invasive mMRI is a promising marker of neuroinflammation in AD.

Neuronal and glial transcriptional states vary with proximity to tangle-bearing neurons in the human entorhinal cortex🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-01 · Boulger, S. L., Lambie, N., Wos, J. … Duff, E. P., Mallach, A., Matthews, P. M.（共 13 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract)
More neurons die in Alzheimer's disease than form tangles, and tangle-bearing neurons can persist for decades, so tangles alone cannot explain degeneration. We combined 6,000-plex spatial transcriptomics with PHF1 immunofluorescence (167,176 cells; 9 donors) and imaging mass cytometry (170,195 cells; 62 donors), measuring each cell's distance to the nearest tangle. Tangle-bearing layer 2-3 excitatory neurons were enriched late along a trajectory of increasing microtubule-associated, synaptic and V-ATPase expression, with elevated p62 and K48-ubiquitin. Tangle-free neurons shared more of this state the closer they lay to a tangle, including higher chaperone and autophagy scores, p62 and nuclear size. Nearby glia had higher disease-associated scores, with astrocytes expressing less GFAP. Excitatory, but not inhibitory, neuron loss was greatest in subtypes closest to tangles and cortical depths richest in phosphorylated tau. Selective neuronal vulnerability may reflect not only a neuron's susceptibility to forming a tangle but also where it lies relative to one.

DNA polymerase kappa supports repair-associated DNA synthesis and genome maintenance in postmitotic neurons🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-01 · Goyal, G., Palanisamy, P., Paul, A. … Cifani, P., Paul, A., Paul, S.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Postmitotic neurons depend on continuous DNA repair to preserve genome integrity, yet the polymerases supporting repair-associated DNA synthesis remain poorly defined. Here, we identify DNA polymerase kappa (POLK), a Y-family translesion synthesis polymerase studied predominantly in proliferating cells, as a regulator of DNA synthesis and genome maintenance in postmitotic neurons. Using primary mouse cortical neurons and human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived forebrain neurons, we show that POLK localizes near newly synthesized DNA and repair-associated proteins, including ubiquitinated PCNA. POLK depletion reduces DNA synthesis in both mouse and human neurons and promotes nuclear and organelle remodeling, p21-associated senescence-like stress, inflammatory responses, and increased p38 MAPK immunoreactivity. In an Alzheimer's disease-risk genetic background, POLK depletion also increases cytoplasmic double-stranded DNA and tau phosphorylation. Conversely, POLK overexpression enhances neuronal DNA synthesis while reducing DNA damage-associated signals, p38 MAPK immunoreactivity, cytoplasmic DNA, and Alzheimer's disease-relevant phenotypes. These findings uncover a role for POLK in postmitotic neuronal genome maintenance, linking specialized DNA polymerase function to neuronal stress and vulnerability relevant to aging and neurodegeneration.
