Explainable 3D CNNs Yield Progression-Linked Profiles of Saliency in Early Parkinson's Disease Using T1-Weighted MRI🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-13 · Moroze, E., Zikopoulos, B., Yazdanbakhsh, A.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Parkinson's Disease (PD) affects 1-2% of adults over 65 and is diagnosed by tracking motor symptoms, underscoring the need for earlier detection. T1-weighted MRI (T1w MRI) is inexpensive and widely available but can lack the resolution to detect established markers of PD pathology, such as substantia nigra degeneration. We trained 3D convolutional neural networks (CNNs) on five site-stratified folds of 249 T1w MRIs from 141 early PD subjects (<10 years since first symptoms) and 185 from 126 controls. Because every fold was evaluated only on sites absent from its training data, the ensemble accuracy of 67.8% (AUC 0.670) reflects generalization to unseen sites. To interrogate these decisions, we parcellated saliency from a cohort-contrastive GradCAM, which baselines PD-class saliency against controls, and from Integrated Gradients, using the Julich Brain atlas. Both showed saliency in neocortical areas matching later Braak stages (5-6), when pathology reaches the neocortex, and lower subcortical saliency, suggesting limited sensitivity to earlier stages. Saliency was more correlated within functional networks than among random region groupings, and pooled saliency tracked motor symptoms and medication rather than cognitive scores or duration. These findings show explainable 3D CNNs can identify meaningful network and regional signatures of early PD from T1w MRI.

The adaptor protein DAB2 cell-autonomously links APOE with disease-associated microglia (DAM)/TREM2 state dysregulation associated with Alzheimer's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-13 · Salih, D. A., Barber, M., Andrews, W. D. … Howell, B. W., Parnavelas, J., Hardy, J.（共 22 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
The neuron-secreted Reelin (RELN) protein and its adaptor DAB1 modify Alzheimer's disease (AD) risk through genetic interaction with APOE4. While RELN and APOE4 exert antagonistic effects on amyloid and Tau pathology in mice, how risk is conferred in the human brain remains unclear. The related adaptor DAB2 endocytoses RELN/APOE receptors and, via LRP8, modulates non-canonical pro-inflammatory signalling in non-neuronal cells. We hypothesised that RELN and APOE signalling directly influence microglial function to modify AD risk. Integrating human brain transcriptomics, a Reln-deficient mouse, and DAB1/DAB2 knock-down in APOE isogenic iPSC-microglia, we found that microglial DAB1/DAB2 pathways were dynamically regulated across the lifespan, with DAB2 dysregulated with increasing amyloid pathology. DAB2 transcriptional networks correlated with disease- and lipid- associated microglial states (DAM/LDAM) linked to networks expressing dementia-risk loci (TREM2, GRN, ACE, SORL1) and associated with endolysosomal and lipid-handling pathways. DAB2- knockdown cell-autonomously shifted microglia away from gene networks enriched in DAM/TREM2 states and towards pro-inflammatory cytokine-responsive states and TNFNF{kappa}B pathways. APOE4/E4 microglia showed elevated APOE and reduced DAB1pY232 under inflammatory stress, and blunted DAB2-knockdown transcriptomic responses, consistent with pathway-level genetic interaction. In vivo, RELN-deficiency reduced microglial ramification and increased CD68. These findings identify DAB2 as a regulator of microglial state transitions and a modifier of dementia-risk networks linking APOE genotype to inflammatory, phagocytic and amyloid-beta clearance pathways relevant to AD.

Peptide-centric proteomics reveals molecular subtypes of sporadic Alzheimer's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-13 · Plubell, D. L. L., Merrihew, G. E. E., Park, J. … Keene, C. D., Montine, T. J., MacCoss, M. J.（共 10 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中title/abstract)
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a complex, age-related disease that continues to increase in incidence worldwide. Although some cases are caused by known pathogenic mutations, the majority of cases are sporadic, with uncertain etiology and commonly complicated by comorbid diseases. To better understand the molecular landscape of AD we constructed a unique brain sample set free from neuropathologic comorbidities. This cohort consists of autosomal dominant AD dementia, sporadic AD dementia, cognitively normal individuals with high AD neuropathologic change - a condition called resilient to AD, and cognitively normal cases with no or minimal AD neuropathologic change. Through quantitative mass spectrometry proteomics we obtain measures of peptides mapping to A-beta; and to tau, capturing differences in the histopathologic burden of disease. Correlations of these peptide abundance differences within sporadic AD dementia cases in the superior and medial temporal gyrus indicate the presence of two separate sporadic AD phenotypes. Additional differences are characterized at a peptide level between sporadic AD dementia and autosomal dominant AD dementia in the inferior parietal lobe and caudate nucleus. These findings indicate both shared and distinct molecular features in AD influenced by cause and age of onset of disease.

Single-nucleus RNA-seq atlas of 5xFAD mouse brain maps cell-type-specific transcriptional disruption and preferential rolipram rescue in VIP interneurons🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-13 · Potokiri, A., Dwamena, A. A., Wang, H.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Background: Alzheimer's disease is characterized by progressive synaptic dysfunction and inhibitory circuit disruption across multiple brain cell types. Rolipram, a selective phosphodiesterase 4 (PDE4) inhibitor that elevates intracellular cyclic AMP (cAMP), activates proteasome, and produces robust procognitive effects in AD mouse models. Still, the cell-type-specific transcriptional mechanisms underlying these effects remain uncharacterized. Methods: We profiled 29,664 nuclei from 5xFAD and wild-type (WT) mouse right hemisphere across four conditions: WT Vehicle, WT Rolipram, 5xFAD Vehicle, and 5xFAD Rolipram (0.03 mg/kg/day, 30-day subcutaneous osmotic pump) using the Parse Biosciences Evercode WT v3 combinatorial barcoding platform. Nuclei were clustered into 16 annotated cell type populations using Seurat v5. Disease and rescue effects were quantified as log fold changes (LFCs) across all clusters, with Gene Ontology enrichment assessed using clusterProfiler and transcription factor activity inferred using decoupleR. Results: Disease-associated transcriptional disruption was highly cell-type-specific in its magnitude. Among inhibitory subtypes, VIP/Reln disinhibitory interneurons (n = 149 nuclei) showed the most coherent transcriptional disruption: 289 candidate genes were lower in 5xFAD, with Gene Ontology enrichment concentrated in synapse organization programs, including regulation of synapse structure or activity (padj = 1.5x10^-14, 44 genes), regulation of synapse organization (padj = 1.9x10^-14, 43 genes), and postsynapse organization (padj = 1.1x10^-13, 38 genes). This pathway-level specificity was not reproduced in PVALB or SST interneurons. Astrocytes showed loss of cell-adhesion programs (cell-substrate adhesion padj = 2.4x10^-4, 10 genes). Rolipram rescue was preferential: VIP interneurons yielded 72 true rescue genes and astrocytes yielded 42, with the remaining 14 clusters showing substantially fewer. The cAMP-specific phosphodiesterase Pde7b was disease-context-specifically lower in 5xFAD VIP interneurons (LFC -0.53) and restored by rolipram (LFC +0.54), with WT expression unchanged. decoupleR CREB1 activity inference identified reduced CREB1 activity in 5xFAD VIP interneurons with partial restoration in 5xFAD Rolipram, supporting a stepwise transcriptional cascade in which amyloid pathology reduces cAMP, which suppresses PKA and CREB1 activity. This in turn reduces Pde7b transcription, and rolipram reverses this cascade by blocking cAMP hydrolysis upstream. Conclusion: This single-nucleus atlas provides the first cell-type-resolved characterization of rolipram's transcriptional effects in the 5xFAD brain. VIP disinhibitory interneurons emerge as the most transcriptionally vulnerable inhibitory subtype and the preferential site of rolipram-associated rescue, with the Pde7b/CREB1 signaling axis nominating a tractable, disease-context-specific pharmacological substrate.

Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively vulnerable circuits🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-13 · McGregor, J. N., Fosque, L. J., Brunwasser, S. J. … Shew, W. L., Holtzman, D. M., Hengen, K. B.（共 13 位作者）
关键词『tau、amyloid』(命中title)
Mechanistically distinct neurodegenerative diseases result in similar cognitive deficits, and pathology is often concentrated in a set of circuits known to be selectively vulnerable. Selective vulnerability is an intrinsic property of a circuit: in APP/PS1 mice, where plaques are global, functional disruptions are confined to circuits within the default mode network. What characterizes the dynamics of vulnerable circuits from those of spared circuits is unknown. We asked whether the distinguishing property is the proximity of population dynamics to criticality, a homeostatic set-point of network activity that determines computational capacity. We recorded single units simultaneously in three selectively vulnerable regions (retrosplenial cortex, anterior cingulate cortex, and CA1 hippocampus) and in primary visual cortex of freely behaving young and old APP/PS1 and wild-type mice. Firing rate and spike-time variability differed by genotype inconsistently across regions and ages. In contrast, three measures from criticality theory each revealed deficits that emerged only with age and only in the vulnerable regions. No measure detected a progressive change in V1. Avalanche scaling is not fixed in neural activity, but varies along a scaling line as the network state changes. As a result, the lines generated by each genotype revealed a constitutive effect of the transgene in young animals, which inverted with age in amyloid-bearing animals, but only in vulnerable circuits. If the destabilization of near-critical dynamics directly reflects a circuit's functional capacity, the same signature should identify a mechanistically distinct degenerative disease. In a model of tauopathy (P301S/E4), CA1 dynamics were displaced from the control scaling line with the same age-dependent reversal observed in APP/PS1. Our results suggest a network-level principle of neurodegenerative diseases: molecularly unrelated pathologies converge on the loss of near-critical dynamics in selectively vulnerable circuits.

The TRIM32 granule promotes TDP-43 inclusion formation and amyloid aggregation through shuttle condensates🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Meng, F., He, Z., Zhou, J. … Troncoso, J. C., Worley, P. F., Zhang, W.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyotrophic lateral sclerosis、amyloid』(命中abstract/title)
Aberrant protein aggregation and inclusion formation are hallmarks of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and frontotemporal lobar degeneration (FTLD), which share overlapping genetic and pathological features. Similar aggregates are increasingly recognized in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and limbic-predominant age-related TDP-43 encephalopathy (LATE). However, it remains unclear whether a shared molecular pathway drives this pathological aggregation. Here, we report the formation of stress-responsive cytoplasmic TRIM32 granules, distinct from stress granules. The E3 ubiquitin ligase TRIM32, together with the shuttle factor UBQLN2 and the autophagy adaptor p62/SQSTM1, drive condensation of TRIM32 granules that depend on E3 ligase activity and a network of intermolecular interactions. These condensates act as scaffolds that capture client proteins, including TDP-43 and ANXA11, and modulate their mobility. A unique hydrophobic loop within TRIM32's substrate-binding domain mimics low-complexity motifs in ANXA11 and TDP-43, enabling selective retention via competitive binding mediated by the UBQLN2 STI1 domain. Moreover, TRIM32 granules promote amyloid aggregation of TDP-43, an effect that is exacerbated by a pathogenic UBQLN2 mutation. In brains from individuals with diverse neurodegenerative diseases, TRIM32 co-localizes with pathological phospho-TDP-43 (pTDP-43) inclusions, supporting a model that TRIM32-driven granules function as selective proteostasis sorting compartments that broadly contribute to TDP-43 proteinopathy.

Sleep promotes neuronal autophagy for intracellular clearance🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Yang, L., Zhang, X., Li, J., Liu, X., Liu, D.
关键词『amyloid』(命中abstract)
Sleep is essential for brain health, yet how it maintains neuronal homeostasis at the cellular level remains poorly understood. Here, we show that extended wakefulness selectively suppressed neuronal autophagic flux in the cortex of mice, whereas sleep rapidly restored it, accompanied by a shift in autophagic cargo from mitochondrial components to synaptic proteins. Sleep upregulated ATG7 expression in the cortex, involving both lysosomal degradation and a putative noncanonical secretory route. Moreover, low neuronal activity is associated with efficient flux, as Gq-mediated neuronal activation impaired autophagy and promoted intracellular amyloid-beta; accumulation. Notably, closed-loop optogenetic stimulation of lysosomal acidification, exclusively during sleep, reduced amyloid pathology in 5xFAD mice, whereas wake-targeted stimulation worsened pathology. Together, these findings demonstrate that sleep serves as a physiological gatekeeper for cortical neuronal autophagy, and highlight the importance of brain-state timing for enhancing intracellular proteostasis in neurodegenerative disease.

An AAV model of tauopathy targeting the mouse dentate gyrus reveals greater trans-synaptic tau spreading with aggregation-prone tau variants🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Leal, P., Duwat, C., Adamski, C. … Bonvento, G., Cambon, K., Bemelmans, A.-P.（共 16 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract/title)
Alzheimer's disease is characterized by progressive memory and cognitive decline, which is strongly correlated with the anatomical progression of tau pathology. Resulting from the intraneuronal aggregation of misfolded tau protein, tauopathy propagates in an anterograde manner between synaptically connected neurons through a prion-like mechanism. While glial cells, particularly microglia, are implicated in this process, the precise mechanisms underlying tauopathy propagation remain poorly understood. A critical unresolved question is whether aggregated or soluble pathologic tau forms mediate this spread. Here, we show in vivo that tau spreading scales with the aggregation propensity of the tau variant expressed. We developed a mouse model using adeno-associated virus (AAV)-mediated gene transfer to overexpress distinct tau forms in the dentate gyrus, alongside a reporter gene. This design enabled discrimination between transduced neurons and those secondarily affected by pathology propagation. Leveraging contralateral neuronal projections, we quantified propagation levels. To investigate the role of microglia and of extracellular vesicles, we analyzed microglial morphology in response to different tau forms and measured tau protein levels in extracellular vesicles isolated from injected mouse brains. We first confirmed the presence of vector-derived tau protein in non-transduced neurons, validating our model for studying tau propagation. We then found that aggregation-prone tau variants were associated with a greater number of tau-positive neurons in the contralateral hippocampus than wild-type tau. Additionally, extracellular vesicles from brains injected with aggregated tau contained higher levels of tau protein. Given that insoluble tau aggregates represent the ultimate stage of tauopathy, our findings suggest that propagation occurs at advanced stages of tau pathology, and that extracellular vesicles may contribute to the transfer of pathological tau, although the cellular origin of these vesicles was not determined in this study. Furthermore, our model provides a robust experimental paradigm for exploring therapeutic strategies aimed at inhibiting tauopathy propagation.

PKR drives neuropathology in a mouse model of Leigh syndrome🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Gonzalez-Torres, M., Bianchi, P., Blanco-Ramos, M. … Prigione, A., Sanz, E., Quintana, A.（共 24 位作者）
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Mitochondrial dysfunction is increasingly recognized as a trigger of innate immune responses, yet whether these responses contribute to neurodegeneration remains poorly understood. Here, we uncover a cell-type-specific antiviral program in vulnerable GABAergic neurons of Ndufs4-deficient mice and human brain organoids modeling Leigh syndrome. Despite robust type I interferon responses, genetic ablation of innate immune mediators Ifnar1 or Sting1 fails to ameliorate disease progression. Instead, we identify the double-stranded RNA sensor protein kinase R (PKR) as a critical mediator of mitochondrial neuropathology. PKR associates with mitochondrial RNA, while its genetic deletion or silencing delays neurological decline, attenuates neuroinflammation, and extends survival without restoring mitochondrial respiration. PKR deletion also normalizes JNK signaling without detectable global translational repression, suggesting a noncanonical contribution to disease. Together, our findings reveal PKR-mediated antiviral RNA sensing as a pathogenic mechanism in mitochondrial complex I deficiency and highlight PKR as a candidate target for mitochondrial encephalopathies.
