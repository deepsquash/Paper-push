Global weight-Ranked cluster-grOWing (GROW): A Cluster-Growing Framework for Robust Functional Coordinate Network Mapping🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Liu, Y., Fu, X., Wang, X. … Zhang, N., Zhang, Y., Qin, W.（共 21 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Coordinate Network Mapping (CNM) has become an important approach for translating scattered neuroimaging findings into circuit-level models of brain disorders such as Alzheimers disease (AD). But conventional CNM uses fixed-radius spheres and pools heterogeneous studies without accounting for confounders. We developed Global weight-Ranked cluster-grOWing (GROW), which reconstructs clusters behind reported peaks using cluster information and a reference statistical weight map, without original imaging data. We analyzed data from 521 participants and 69 published studies (82 experiments, 5,555 participants). GROW-CNM was compared with fixed-radius CNM (Fixed-CNM) in spatial fidelity, cross-study convergence, sampling stability, and sensitivity to confounders. At study level, Fixed-CNM corresponded poorly with the reference atrophy-derived functional network in AD (Spearman r = -0.24, overlap = 1.61%), and radius changes did not improve fidelity. In contrast, GROW-CNM recovered cluster morphology and generated maps closer to the reference network (r [&ge;] 0.85, overlap [&ge;] 87.30%). GROW-CNM also showed greater cross-study convergence (median peak probability, 0.99 vs. 0.67; z = 27.42, p < 0.001) and was robust to threshold/resampling (z = -169.84, p < 0.001). Fixed-CNM was associated with systematic factors; GROW-CNM was more sensitive to demographic confounders (p < 0.05, TFCE-FWE correction). Systematic confounding was attenuated by GROW-CNM; Fixed-CNM remained susceptible in core regions (p < 0.001). These findings indicate cross-study heterogeneity in coordinate-based AD network maps reflects seed definition and study-level confounders, not biology alone. Combining GROW-based seed reconstruction with confounder-adjusted population modeling may provide a robust framework for mapping disease-related brain networks and prioritizing candidate neuromodulation targets.

关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Motor dysfunction is typically assessed through clinical examination and manual scoring, with gait analysis and accelerometry providing objective measures of subtle abnormalities. The observable 4-6 Hz resting tremor of Parkinsons disease highlights the importance of capturing motor fluctuations at relevant timescales. Using a high-precision industrial weighing module, we tested whether high-frequency weight recordings of freely moving mice could provide a simple, noninvasive means of detecting disease-associated motor abnormalities. Aging MitoPark mice spend more time in non-ambulatory states and exhibit less stable locomotion than healthy controls; however, bradykinesia and shaking can be mitigated by intraperitoneal levodopa. Here we demonstrate that irregular motor activity and tremor-like movements are quantifiable via weight frequency analyses, which permit detection of early symptoms and post-levodopa motor control restoration. This non-invasive approach is less data-intensive than video-based analyses, adaptable for home-cage monitoring, and potentially translatable for clinical or home use for detection of subclinical motor dysfunction.

Loss of Tau in the adult brain causes progressive disruption of neuronal homeostasis and behavioral performance🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增

关键词『tau』(命中title)
Tau has been extensively studied in neurodegenerative disease, yet its physiological role in the adult brain remains elusive. Here, we generated an inducible conditional Tau knockout mouse model to selectively delete Tau in adult brain and investigated the consequences of Tau loss across multiple levels. Tau knock-down in the forebrain induced progressive emotional and cognitive deficits, accompanied by dendritic remodeling, impaired spine maturation, and disrupted neuronal network activity. Region-specific knock-down of Tau in the amygdala was sufficient to reproduce anxiety-like behavior, while Tau re-expression restored normal behavior. Crucially, antisense oligonucleotide-mediated Tau knockdown phenocopied these effects, demonstrating that partial depletion of endogenous Tau is sufficient to impair neuronal integrity and behavior. Together, these findings establish an essential role for physiological Tau in maintaining adult neuronal circuit homeostasis and provide important insight into the consequences of sustained Tau-lowering interventions.

关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title)
Alzheimers disease (AD), characterized by the accumulation of amyloid {beta}-peptide (A{beta}) plaques, is a progressive age-related neurodegenerative disorder affecting more than 35 million people worldwide. The risk of developing AD is significantly influenced by genetic factors. Deciphering their molecular functions has substantially advanced our understanding of the pathogenic mechanisms underlying AD. The PICALM gene represents a highly validated AD risk factor, and the encoded endocytic adaptor CALM is critical for endothelial A{beta} clearance and microglial A{beta} phagocytosis. Dopamine shifts striatal ramping dynamics🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Bova, A. S., Volkman, R. A., Khandelwal, V. … Dawson, J. D., Uc, E. Y., Narayanan, N. S.（共 12 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Diseases that disrupt dopamine impair cognition; however, it is unknown how dopamine affects neuronal networks that instantiate cognitive processing. We studied this problem in the dorsomedial striatum during interval timing, an elementary cognitive task requiring working memory and attention to estimate an interval of several seconds. Interval timing requires medium spiny neurons (MSNs) that ramp up or down over temporal intervals. While these neurons receive prominent dopaminergic input from the substantia nigra, it is unknown how dopamine modulates MSN activity during interval timing. Here, we report four new findings. First, ramping activity of MSN ensembles predicts interval timing behavior. Second, dopamine dynamics predict timing behavior and preferentially predict the slope of MSN ensembles that ramp down; these relationships are not observed during tasks without temporal control such as Pavlovian conditioning. Third, inactivating substantia nigra dopamine disrupts timing as well as MSNs that ramp down, but not those that ramp up. Fourth, a drift-diffusion model perturbing down-ramping accurately predicted interval timing behavior in mice with dopamine inhibition and in humans with Parkinson's disease. Together, these results reveal how dopamine shapes striatal ramping dynamics and suggest that disrupting dopamine can alter specific trajectories of cognitive computations. These data are relevant for understanding the cognitive striatum and for generating new markers and therapeutic strategies for human striatal and dopaminergic diseases.

Conditional feature importance with error control for machine-learning-based biomarker discovery in Alzheimer's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Paillard, J., Silvestri, E., Bryois, J. … Engemann, D. A., for the Swedish BioFINDER study group,, for the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative,（共 15 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is characterized by complex biology captured by proteomics, transcriptomics, genetics, and brain imaging. These high-dimensional and correlated data make biomarker discovery challenging. To disentangle biomedical AD data, we propose Conditional Feature Importance (CFI), a theory-grounded framework quantifying the unique contribution of individual or grouped variables in any machine learning model while controlling false discoveries. Across ADNI, BioFINDER, and UK Biobank (nearly 48,000 participants), CFI uncovered a striking contrast between unique versus redundant biomarkers across protein and imaging signatures of AD diagnosis, onset, and progression. APOE {varepsilon}4 offered no unique information once downstream proteins were accounted for, whereas contextual markers such as CSF A{beta}40 emerged only through interactions with other biomarkers. We argue that CFI increases the scientific value of machine learning by providing rigorous statistical insights on complex datasets.

A Cross-Tissue Multi-Omics Atlas of Brain Transcriptomes and Liquid Biopsy EV Proteomes in Parkinson's Disease and Multiple System Atrophy🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Lee, E. H., Lee, Y., Jo, K. B. … Kim, J.-s., Kim, J.-E., Kim, D.-K.（共 20 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、neurodegeneration』(命中title/abstract)
Parkinson's disease (PD) and Multiple System Atrophy (MSA) are progressive synucleinopathies characterized by widespread molecular alterations across neuronal and glial populations. While brain tissue provides direct insight into disease mechanisms, liquid biopsies, particularly extracellular vesicles (EVs) in plasma, cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), and urine, offer a minimally invasive avenue for clinical diagnosis and monitoring. However, the extent to which cell-type-specific brain transcriptional changes translate into detectable circulating protein signatures remains unclear. Here, we present a comprehensive multi-omics meta-analysis integrating eight human post-mortem brain single-nucleus RNA-sequencing (snRNA-seq) datasets with eight circulating proteomic datasets. We uniformly reprocessed and analyzed over 1 million nuclei, spanning ~20,000 protein-coding genes across four broadly defined disease-relevant brain regions from 171 individuals alongside ~8,000 liquid biopsy proteins from over 400 individuals. We resolved cell-type-specific differentially expressed genes (DEGs) across dopaminergic neurons, microglia, astrocytes, and oligodendrocytes alongside disease-specific differentially expressed proteins (DEPs) in peripheral biofluids. Mapping brain DEGs onto liquid biopsy proteomes revealed that ~25% of brain DEGs were detectable as biofluid DEPs in at least one dataset, while multi-cohort filtering prioritized 32 unique recurrent cross-omic biomarkers. These include concordantly regulated markers of complement and glial reactivity (C1QB, C3, CHI3L1), neurotrophic and neuropeptide signaling (IGFBP5, NRP1, SST), lipid metabolism (LPL, APOC1), proteostasis (HSP90B1), and cytoskeletal/extracellular matrix remodeling (MMP2, PLOD1), alongside inversely regulated chaperones and synaptic/vascular factors (HSP90AA1, CAMK2A, VWF). Furthermore, cross-omic protein-protein interaction networks converged on pathways governing chaperone-mediated protein folding, ubiquitin-proteasome degradation, mitochondrial bioenergetics, and SNARE-mediated vesicular transport. Ultimately, this integrative atlas links brain cell-type-specific transcriptomic dysregulation to accessible EV cargo, providing a scalable framework for mechanistically informed liquid biopsies in neurodegeneration.

Target-dependent modulation of finely tuned gamma by transcranial ultrasound in Parkinson's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Asadian, A., Sarica, C., Ramezanpour, H. … Chen, R., Lozano, A. M., Darmani, G.（共 21 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Background: Finely tuned gamma (FTG) is a narrowband oscillation associated with dopaminergic ON states and dyskinesia in Parkinson's disease (PD), although its physiological significance remains debated. While deep brain stimulation (DBS) can entrain gamma oscillations, whether noninvasive ultrasound can modulate endogenous FTG across motor circuits remains unclear. Methods: We analyzed local field potentials recorded using sensing-enabled DBS systems in two cohorts of patients: 17 with subthalamic nucleus (STN) and 10 with globus pallidus internus (GPi) electrodes. Theta-burst transcranial ultrasound stimulation (tbTUS) targeted the primary motor cortex (M1) or GPi. FTG was assessed before, during, and up to 45 minutes after sonication. Associations with beta activity, levodopa-equivalent daily dose (LEDD), and dyskinesia were also examined. Results: FTG was detected in eight STN patients and one GPi patient, with five exhibiting reproducible peaks suitable for longitudinal analysis, broadly consistent with previous reports of intermittent FTG expression. M1 sonication reduced STN FTG in three patients, whereas GPi sonication increased STN FTG in one but did not significantly alter locally recorded GPi FTG in another. Finger-tapping-related FTG responses were heterogeneous. Resting FTG was not significantly correlated with beta activity or LEDD. However, dyskinesia was more frequent in sessions with detectable FTG than in those without (47% vs. 20%), although this association was not statistically significant (p = 0.067). Conclusions: These findings suggest that noninvasive ultrasound can modulate endogenous FTG in a circuit-dependent manner, distinct from DBS-induced gamma entrainment. FTG may provide a complementary electrophysiological biomarker alongside beta oscillations, potentially capturing additional aspects of motor-network activity and dyskinesia. Combining noninvasive TUS with intracranial DBS sensing may offer a useful approach for investigating FTG and its potential application in future adaptive DBS and individualized neuromodulation strategies.

Human lipidomics guides brain-inspired lipoprotein particle approach to ameliorate Alzheimer's disease pathology🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Pinals, R. L., Koh, Y., Ke, Z. … Bennett, D. A., Clish, C. B., Tsai, L.-H.（共 30 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
APOE encodes the principal lipid-transporting protein in the brain, and lipid dysregulation is increasingly implicated in Alzheimer's disease (AD) pathogenesis. The APOE4 allele, together with advanced age and female sex, are major risk factors for late-onset AD. Yet how these risk factors interact to reshape lipid composition and transport in the brain remains unclear. Herein, we characterize lipidomic profiles in the brain, cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), and blood of individuals with or without AD, as well as of mice expressing humanized APOE. We identify brain lipidomic shifts associated with AD in humans, including relative depletion of highly unsaturated lipids, with many changes resembling those associated with APOE4 alone. Individuals with AD show striking depletion of lipoprotein particle (LP)-associated lipid abundance in the CSF. Additionally, humanized APOE4 mice exhibit an age-dependent deficit in CSF LP-associated lipid abundance relative to APOE3 mice. Accordingly, we leverage these insights to engineer brain-inspired reconstituted LPs (rLPs) with the goal of restoring lipid transport in the brain. rLPs promote cholesterol efflux from human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived APOE4 astrocytes in vitro. In an AD mouse model (5xFAD) expressing humanized APOE4, these nanoparticles reduce lipid-droplet accumulation, facilitate amyloid-beta clearance, enhance synaptic integrity, and improve cognitive function. Together, our results provide a resource mapping lipidomic changes across the Alzheimer's risk triad and demonstrate that engineered rLPs can counter lipid accumulation and associated pathology in the brain, and cognitive deficits in vivo.

Neuronal heparan sulfate controls the brain accumulation and glymphatic clearance of pathogenic α-synuclein🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Digraskar, S., Das, P., Bash, R. … Sorace, A., Volpicelli-Daley, L., Aguilar-Calvo, P.（共 18 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
In Parkinson's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies, fibrillar aggregates of the -synuclein (-syn) protein are released into the extracellular space and drive disease progression, yet the mechanisms governing their in vivo neuronal uptake and clearance remain poorly understood. Heparan sulfate proteoglycans (HSPGs) act as receptors for -syn fibril internalization in iPSC-derived dopaminergic neurons and could therefore advance synucleinopathy progression in patients. Here we show that -syn fibrils interact electrostatically with the sulfate groups in the HS chains with high affinity, and that genetically reducing the sulfation of neuronal HS reduces the accumulation of -syn aggregates in multiple brain areas, the loss of dopaminergic neurons, and the development of a locomotor behavioral phenotype in mice intracerebrally injected with -syn recombinant fibrils (PFFs). In vivo imaging revealed that HS is also a molecular obstacle to the glymphatic clearance of -syn fibrils, as radiolabeled -syn PFFs travel faster through the brain towards cerebrospinal clearance in mice with less sulfated neuronal HS than in wild-type mice. Collectively, this study provides in vivo evidence that neuronal HS accelerates -syn pathology, potentially by promoting neuronal uptake while slowing the glymphatic clearance of -syn fibrils and, therefore, is a promising therapeutic target to halt synucleinopathies.

bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-27 · Jiang, R., Johansson, L., Tambaro, S. … Gouras, G. K., Hallbeck, M., Nilsson, P.（共 23 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、amyloid』(命中abstract/title)
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is characterized by amyloid {beta} (A{beta}) deposition, tau aggregation and impaired proteostasis, including autophagy dysfunction. However, how autophagy deficiency affects A{beta} pathology and disease progression remains unclear. We crossed excitatory neuronspecific Atg7 knockout mice with App knock-in mice (AppNL-F/NL-F and AppNL-G-F/NL-G-F) and assessed A{beta} pathology, extracellular vesicle (EV) release, cognition, gamma oscillatory activity, cell death, brain atrophy and proteomic changes. Autophagy deficiency reduced extracellular A{beta} plaque burden despite increased release of A{beta}-containing EVs. Notably, combined Arctic-A{beta} pathology and autophagy deficiency impaired gamma oscillatory activity, behaviour and memory, activated cell death signalling and shortened lifespan. Surprisingly, less pronounced A{beta} pathology counteracted several detrimental effects, potentially through improved cellular transport and reduced protein translation. These findings reveal that autophagy regulates A{beta} metabolism and that A{beta} pathology severity determines the consequences of autophagy deficiency, highlighting autophagy modulation as a context-dependent therapeutic target in AD.

bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-26 · Riebenbauer, B., Hoffmann, F., Galicia, C. … Boldt, K., Versees, W., Gloeckner, C. J.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Leucine-rich repeat kinase 2 (LRRK2) is a multidomain signaling protein implicated in Parkinson's disease whose activity is closely linked to conformational state. How these structural transitions shape the cellular molecular environment of LRRK2 remains poorly understood. Here, we combined nanobody-mediated conformational control with miniTurbo proximity proteomics to define molecular neighborhoods associated with inhibitory, neutral, and activating LRRK2 states. Structural modeling suggested that the activating nanobody Nb22 targets the WD40 domain and destabilizes interactions maintaining the autoinhibited conformation. Consistent with this model, activation was accompanied by ordered, reciprocal remodeling of the LRRK2 proximity proteome, with progressive enrichment of centrosomal, ciliary, and RAB/endolysosomal machinery and depletion of soluble cytosolic, proteostasis-associated and 14-3-3 proteins. Notably, the Nb22-associated proteome combined centrosomal and endolysosomal components previously linked to distinct LRRK2 signaling environments. These findings support a model in which conformational transitions reorganize the spatial molecular neighborhood of LRRK2 and thereby couple kinase state to distinct cellular signaling networks.

Striatal lateral inhibition regulates abnormal movements in a mouse model of levodopa-induced dyskinesia🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-26 · Twedell, E. L., Barnhill, O. K., Bair-Marshall, C. J. … Scaria, L. K., Sridhar, S., Nelson, A. B.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Striatal medium spiny neurons (MSNs) integrate convergent cortical, thalamic, and dopaminergic inputs to shape motor output. In addition, MSNs form local inhibitory synaptic connections with one another. Striatal lateral inhibition may contribute to the regulation of motor output, but its role in pathological motor function remains incompletely understood. Disruptions in motor function characterize several movement disorders, including levodopa-induced dyskinesia (LID), a complication of Parkinsons disease (PD) therapy characterized by involuntary movements. Here, we identify chronic changes in the strength of striatal lateral inhibitory synapses in a mouse model of PD/LID. These synapses are also modulated by acute dopamine signaling. Chemogenetic suppression of lateral inhibition originating from dopamine D2 receptor-expressing MSNs lowers the threshold for the expression of involuntary movements in vivo. Together, these findings identify a contribution of striatal lateral inhibition to motor dysfunction and suggest a broader role for this microcircuit in regulating motor output.

Estradiol enhances sensory-evoked dilation of cortical penetrating arterioles in a mouse model of menopause despite pre-existing metabolic dysfunction🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-26 · Plumley, Z. M., Calvo Iglesias, J., Fernandez Ugidos, I. … Taylor, H., Ruscher, J. B., Mostany, R.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Estrogen loss during menopause is a risk factor for cardiometabolic and neurovascular dysfunction, cognitive decline, and Alzheimers disease. Rodent studies of menopause have shown neuroprotective effects of 17{beta}-estradiol (E2) treatment when administered within a critical period, though these benefits have not been fully demonstrated in clinical trials of hormone therapy. A proposed explanation for this mismatch is the "healthy cell bias," where estrogen is only beneficial when initiated in physiologically intact systems. Our study investigates whether pre-existing metabolic dysfunction attenuates the effects of E2 on neurovascular coupling (NVC) in a rodent model of menopause. Female mice were fed 11 weeks of high-fat diet (HFD) or control diet (CD) to induce metabolic disease, followed by ovariectomy (OVX) and E2 or vehicle (Veh) treatment. NVC was assessed in awake mice using two-photon laser scanning microscopy of penetrating arterioles (PAs) in the somatosensory cortex, barrel field. Mice developed glucose intolerance and increased adiposity yet displayed intact NVC following HFD exposure, and subsequent OVX impaired NVC irrespective of metabolic health status. However, E2 treatment enhanced NVC responses regardless of diet relative to controls. These results indicate that estrogens effects on NVC are preserved in HFD+OVX conditions, which has implications for the use of hormone therapy in individuals that arrive at the menopause transition with pre-existing cardiometabolic disease.

Age-dependent brain pigmentation drives early neuroinflammatory molecular signatures linked to neurodegeneration🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-26 · Penuelas, N., Xicoy, H., Lorente-Picon, M. … Hoyo, J., Laguna, A., Vila, M.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract/title)
Background: Neuromelanin (NM) is a pigment that progressively accumulates with age in catecholaminergic neurons, particularly in the substantia nigra, ventral tegmental area, and locus coeruleus. These neuronal populations are especially vulnerable to degeneration in Parkinson s disease (PD). Elevated intracellular NM levels have been linked to neurodegeneration and PD-like phenotypes in experimental models. However, the molecular mechanisms underlying NM-induced pathology remain poorly understood, as human studies cannot disentangle the specific effects of NM accumulation from those of normal aging. Methods: We performed transcriptomic microarray analysis on laser-captured catecholaminergic neurons and regions (substantia nigra, ventral tegmental area, locus coeruleus) from NM-producing transgenic mice (tgNM) and NM-free wild-type controls across different ages, and compared them to data from postmortem human brain tissue. One of the molecular targets identified, GPNMB, was validated in mouse and human tissue, and functionally tested in vivo. Results: We identified region- and age-dependent transcriptional changes associated with progressive NM accumulation. NM consistently upregulated neuroinflammatory pathways with enrichment of disease-associated microglial genes, while downregulating transcription, translation, and mitochondrial functions. Locus coeruleus exhibited the earliest and strongest transcriptional alterations, whereas substantia nigra and ventral tegmental area showed a later-onset, age-progressive transcriptional dysfunction. Neuron-specific analyses revealed that many changes originated within NM-containing neurons rather than being solely glial-driven. NM-driven transcriptional profiles in mice strongly correlated with postmortem data from PD patients, underscoring their translational relevance. Among molecular targets, the glycoprotein GPNMB was consistently upregulated in NM-containing neurons and validated at RNA and protein levels in both NM-producing transgenic mice and in human PD brains. Functional experiments demonstrated that GPNMB overexpression attenuated NM-linked dopaminergic neurodegeneration and improved motor performance in mice. Conclusion: This study provides a comprehensive in vivo characterization of NM-specific transcriptomic changes in catecholaminergic neurons, showing that NM accumulation drives neuroinflammatory and neurodegenerative programs. Our results support that the neuroinflammatory changes observed in tgNM mice and in human PD represent early pathological events that precede overt neurodegeneration. The disease-associated gene GPNMB emerged as a conserved NM-induced factor with protective properties, highlighting its potential as a therapeutic target in PD and aging-related neurodegeneration.
