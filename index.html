The TREM-receptor family interactome in the human brain🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · de Kleijn, K., Weller, S., Klaassen, R., Koopmans, F., Smit, A., Wijdeven, R.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
The Triggering Receptors Expressed on Myeloid cells (TREMs) are a family of cell-surface receptors instrumental in the activation of different myeloid cells. TREM2, its most studied member, is genetically linked to Alzheimer disease (AD), and its interactions with BAmyloid; and APOE have placed it at the forefront of microglia dysfunction in the brain. However, the ligands and binding partners of the other TREM members (TREM1, TREML1, TREML2 and TREML4) are less well defined. Here, we used human AD and non-AD brain lysate to profile the interactome of the ligand binding domains of the different TREM receptors. Besides identifying known ligands for TREM1 (PGLYRP1), TREM2 (C1QL3, LGALSL) and TREML1 (Fibrinogens), we identified complement components and the apolipoproteins APOA1 and APOA4 as novel interactors of TREML1, and heparan sulfate modified proteins as binding partners for TREML2. Furthermore, similar to TREM2, all the TREMLs interacted with the apolipoproteins APOE and Clusterin. Using in vitro pulldowns with heparin, we confirmed that specifically TREML2 interacted with heparan sulfates, in a manner unaffected by the AD-linked S144G mutation. Together, our proteomics approach identified novel interactors for TREML1 and TREML2, cementing the role of the different TREM-proteins in lipid trafficking and immunity.

Neuroprotective activity of sub-anesthetic ketamine in a preclinical model of Parkinson's disease is not blocked by antagonizing brain-derived neurotrophic factor signaling🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Stopera, C. J., Bartlett, M. J., Bernard, K. … Sherman, S. J., Steece-Collier, K., Falk, T.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Treatment with low-dose ketamine is neuroprotective in rodent models of acute damage, while high doses of ketamine can be neurotoxic. Our group has previously shown that sub-anesthetic ketamine treatment leads to long-term reduction of L-DOPA-induced dyskinesia (LID) via brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF)-signaling in a rat 6-hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA) model of Parkinson's disease (PD). In this report, we investigated if sub-anesthetic ketamine is also neuroprotective in a progressive 6-OHDA model of PD via neurotrophic action through the tropomyosin receptor kinase B (TrkB) receptor. We demonstrated that ketamine-treatment (20 mg/kg, i.p.) prior to striatal administration of the neurotoxin 6-OHDA is neuroprotective, providing near complete protection against development of parkinsonian motor deficits. This robust behavioral efficacy occurred in the presence of only modest preservation of tyrosine hydroxylase (TH) striatal levels (~50-60% loss). Interestingly, based on data from an additional cohort treated with ketamine and ANA-12 (TrkB antagonist) we found that the neuroprotective effects of ketamine are not mediated by TrkB receptors. Semi-quantitative western analysis showed that the relative tyrosine hydroxylase content in the striatum was not significantly increased in ketamine-treated rats compared to vehicle. Unbiased stereology of dopamine neurons in the substantia nigra pars compacta (SNc) revealed no significant differences between any of the groups. The role of BDNF was evaluated with in situ hybridization for RNA of BDNF and TrkB. While BDNF expression was not changed, co-administration of ketamine and ANA-12 led to a significant decrease in TrkB receptor expression compared to ketamine-only on the lesioned but not the intact hemisphere in the SNc. In conclusion, we show that ketamine at the low dose currently pursued for therapeutic benefit for LID led to robust behavioral motor improvement in this rodent PD degeneration model, yet these protective effects were not driven by neurotrophic BDNF-signaling, indicating complex mechanisms are underlying neuroprotective activities of low-dose ketamine.

L-DOPA Rescues Reach Height but Not Trajectory Area in a Bimanual String-Pulling Behavior in Parkinsonian Rats🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Schmit, M. B., Aristieta, A., Caparu, R. … Vishwanath, A., Falk, T., Cowen, S. L.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
While gross motor changes in Parkinson's disease, bradykinesia, hypokinesia, difficulty initiating movement, have been well studied in rodent models of the disease, limb movements, and especially bimanual coordination deficits, are more difficult to study in these animal models. Here we describe a novel bimanual string-pulling task that can be used to generate a rich repertoire of behaviors that require limb and bimanual coordination in rats with unilateral 6-hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA) lesion. We show that 6-OHDA lesion significantly reduces string speed, pulling bout duration, paw speed especially during reaching, and reduces the area of paw trajectories during pulling. However, it does not affect the variability around that movement trajectory. We also show that levodopa (L-DOPA) treatment restores many of these measures but not all. L-DOPA restores paw speed and string-pulling speed and fully restores paw reach height and pulling bout duration, but has no effect on trajectory area. Together, our results demonstrate that dopamine depletion differentially effects features of this bimanual coordination task, that L-DOPA mostly, but not fully, restores string pulling performance, and opens up a new avenue for measuring the effect of Parkinson's disease treatments on motor movement, allowing for better assessment of how effectively treatments restore motor activity.

Integrative drug discovery for Parkinson disease with DIMER🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Guo, S., Yang, X., Dong, Y. … Kerman, E., Li, R., He, J.（共 12 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Translating Parkinson disease (PD) genetics into therapeutic candidates requires approaches that account for interconnected disease mechanisms and their cellular context. Here we present DIMER (Direct and Indirect Module Evaluation for Repurposing and screening), a framework that connects network-based drug repurposing and target-directed virtual screening through shared disease-associated gene sets. Applying DIMER to PD, we integrated genetic risk and mechanism-associated gene sets with drug-target interactions, disease transcriptomes, and single nucleus expression profiles. Network analysis identified 423 repurposing candidates, with 71% of qualifying drug-gene set associations arising through targets outside the corresponding disease gene set. Transcriptional reversal analysis and cell type-resolved networks further distinguished candidates by their predicted effects on disease-associated expression and their cellular context, while target-set separation nominated potentially complementary drug combinations. For target-directed virtual screening, DIMER adapted a pretrained compound-protein interaction model to screening library chemistry and incorporated known-ligand similarity into compound ranking. Library adaptation improved compound-target pair classification performance, whereas ligand-informed ranking increased early enrichment of known active compounds. Consensus screening of 1.82 million compounds against seven PD-associated proteins yielded a shortlist of 22 compounds supported by at least five ranking runs. In a complementary experimental evaluation, four repurposing candidates (polydatin, fedratinib, sorafenib and venlafaxine) attenuated the loss of dopamine neuron marker signals in human midbrain organoids exposed to 6-hydroxydopamine. Together, these findings establish DIMER as a framework for translating disease-associated genes into pharmacological hypotheses and prioritizing candidates for experimental testing.

Repetitive Levodopa Treatment Drives Cell Type-Specific Striatal Adaptations Associated With Progressive Dyskinesia in Parkinsonian Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Paz, R. M., Ryan, M. B., Marcott, P. F. … Duong, V., Sridhar, S., Nelson, A. B.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
The use of levodopa to manage Parkinsons disease (PD) symptoms leads to levodopa-induced dyskinesia (LID) and other motor fluctuations, which worsen with disease progression and repeated treatment. Aberrant activity of striatal D1- and D2-expressing medium spiny neurons (D1-/D2-MSNs) underlies LID, but the mechanisms driving its progression remain unclear. Using the 6-OHDA mouse model of PD/LID, we combined in vivo and ex vivo recordings to isolate the effect of repeated treatment in LID worsening and other changes in the motor response to levodopa. We found that LID worsening is linked to potentiation of levodopa-evoked responses in both D1-/D2-MSNs, independent of changes in dopamine release or MSN intrinsic excitability. Instead, strengthening of glutamatergic synapses onto D1-MSNs emerged as a key signature. Moreover, we found changes in D2-MSN activity are associated with LID duration, which paralleled a reduction in D2R sensitivity. These findings reveal striatal adaptations contributing to worsening of levodopa-related complications.

Alpha-Synuclein Co-Pathology and Amyloid-Tau Coupling in Alzheimer's Disease: Cross-Sectional and Longitudinal Analyses Across Braak Stage Regions🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Negida, A., Rissardo, J. P., Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative,
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、alpha-synuclein、amyloid』(命中title)
**Background:** Misfolded alpha-synuclein (alphaSyn), a common co-pathology in Alzheimer's disease, acts synergistically with amyloid and tau in animal models, but its role in the human amyloid-tau-cognition cascade is unclear. We examined whether alphaSyn modifies the amyloid-tau and tau-cognition associations, cross-sectionally and over time. **Methods:** We analyzed 615 participants from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative with a cerebrospinal fluid alphaSyn seed amplification assay (SAA), flortaucipir tau positron emission tomography (PET) in Braak stage regions, and amyloid PET and domain-specific cognitive composites within 1 year of tau PET. Linear models with heteroscedasticity-consistent standard errors tested alphaSyn x amyloid interactions on regional tau and alphaSyn x tau interactions on cognition; mixed-effects models tested the same interactions on repeated tau PET (n = 341) and cognitive testing (n = 516). **Results:** alphaSyn positivity was present in 117 participants (19.0%). Cross-sectionally, the amyloid-tau association was steeper in alphaSyn SAA+ participants for meta-temporal tau (interaction beta 0.258, 95% CI 0.023 to 0.493; p = 0.032) and Braak I-IV (p <= 0.048); no interaction survived false discovery rate correction (q = 0.072), and clinical-stage adjustment attenuated it (beta 0.114; p = 0.333). Over a median of 3.5 years (n = 341; 68 alphaSyn-positive), no alphaSyn x amyloid x time interaction was detected (meta-temporal beta -0.012, 95% CI -0.046 to 0.023; p = 0.513). No cross-sectional alphaSyn x tau interaction on cognition was detected; at mean tau, alphaSyn positivity was associated with lower executive function (q = 0.032). Longitudinally, tau-related executive decline was less steep in alphaSyn-positive participants (q = 0.042). **Conclusions:** The alphaSyn-associated amyloid-tau interaction was modest, did not withstand multiple-testing correction, and had no detected longitudinal counterpart. alphaSyn positivity was associated with poorer executive function at mean tau; the unexpected longitudinal executive interaction needs replication. These findings do not establish alphaSyn status as a marker for tau-progression enrichment in trials.

Genetic ablation of Sarm1 attenuates axonal degeneration in a mouse model of CMT2J🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-29 · Claessens, A., De Blasis, R., Ferri, C. … Wrabetz, L., Shackleford, G., D'Antonio, M.（共 12 位作者）
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Charcot-Marie-Tooth (CMT) disease is the most common inherited neuropathy, affecting approximately 1 in 2,500 individuals, and comprises a clinically and genetically heterogeneous group of disorders involving more than 100 genes. Mutations in myelin protein zero (MPZ) typically cause the demyelinating form CMT1B, whereas the T124M substitution gives rise to CMT2J, a late-onset, predominantly axonal neuropathy with minimal myelin involvement. We previously generated a MpzT124M mouse model that recapitulates key features of the human disease, including progressive axonal degeneration and fibre loss in peripheral nerves. Because SARM1 (sterile and TIR motif-containing protein 1) is a central executioner of programmed axonal degeneration, we crossed MpzT124M mice with Sarm1-null animals and assessed electrophysiological, morphological, and molecular parameters in both heterozygous and homozygous mutants. Sarm1 deletion did not rescue electrophysiological deficits in either genotype. However, in 12-month-old homozygous MpzT124M/T124M mice, Sarm1 ablation partially reduced axonal degeneration in peripheral nerves and lowered plasma neurofilament light chain, a biomarker of neurodegeneration. In heterozygous MpzT124M/+ mice, where axonal degeneration develops more slowly, Sarm1 deletion was associated with improved motor performance and reduced morphological signs of disease by 18 months of age. Together, these findings indicate that SARM1 contributes to axonal degeneration in CMT2J and that its ablation confers partial protection, although it is insufficient to fully prevent disease progression, supporting SARM1 as a therapeutically relevant target in MPZ-associated axonal neuropathy despite incomplete disease rescue.

Cortical pyramidal tract neurons constrain pathological dopaminergic motor responses🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · Atudorei, M., Olsson, G. E. D., del Agua Villa, C. … Cenci, M. A., Siebner, H., Rickhag, M.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Pyramidal tract (PT) neurons constitute a major cortical output pathway to subcortical structures, yet their contribution to parkinsonian and dyskinetic motor states remains unclear. Here, we show that chemogenetic activation of motor cortical PT neurons selectively confines pathological dopaminergic motor responses. Unilateral PT chemogenetic activation suppressed spontaneous locomotion in healthy mice but did not improve motor deficits following an ipsilateral 6-hydroxydopamine lesion. Instead, it attenuated contraversive rotations provoked by pharmacological D1-receptor stimulation and selectively reduced axial levodopa-induced dyskinesia. Mechanistically, PT stimulation reduced acute D1-mediated c-Fos induction in the dopamine-depleted striatum, without affecting chronic {Delta}FosB accumulation induced by repeated levodopa exposure. These findings identify a functional interaction between cortical PT output and striatal D1-receptor signalling that limits maladaptive motor responses to dopaminergic stimulation. Our results uncover a previously unrecognised cortical mechanism regulating pathological motor behaviour and suggest PT neurons as a potential substrate for circuit-based therapies in Parkinson's disease.

Modelling Audio-Visual Reaction Time with Recurrent Mean-Field Networks🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · Brady, R. M., Butler, J. S.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Audio-visual stimuli are typically detected faster and more accurately than unisensory ones, but it remains unclear whether this benefit reflects true integration of evidence or other effects such as independent processing and modality repetition. We address this with three biologically motivated models of audio-visual integration, built on Wong and Wang model, a mean-field recurrent framework originally developed for unisensory decision-making. The OR model takes in independent unisensory processes through winner-take-all competition, and the SUM model implements full linear summation of sensory evidence. These two sit at opposite ends of the integration spectrum. The REPEAT model adds switch and repeat costs, whereby responses are faster when the modality repeats across trials. We simulated 121 participants whose unisensory evidence accumulation rates varied from modality dominance to balanced integration. We fitted the simulated reaction times and accuracy with a drift diffusion model and compared the resulting multisensory drift rates with the optimal prediction from the unisensory rates, as well as with empirical audio-visual detection data. The OR and SUM Models reproduced established unisensory response patterns from behavioural data from in children, children with autism, adults, older adults, and people with Parkinson's disease. Drift diffusion analysis revealed suboptimal integration in the OR Model and optimal integration in the SUM Model. However, the SUM Model also produced supra-optimal responses under certain conditions, inconsistent with behavioural data. The REPEAT Model captured the role of priming in sensory repetition effects, distinguishing it from true multisensory integration. Together, these models highlight how biologically grounded mathematical frameworks can shed light on the mechanisms underlying multisensory integration, particularly the nuanced contributions of modality repetition and integration efficiency.

Neuronal Hyperactivity Induces Tau Phosphorylation Selectively in Excitatory Pyramidal Neurons Which Contributes to GluA2 Regulation🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · Sun, M. N., Vaughey, K., Liu, B., Guo, H., Huganir, R. L., Morris, M.
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract/title)
While tau plays a role in regulating neuronal network excitability, a process thought to be dysregulated in Alzheimers disease (AD), the molecular mechanisms linking tau and hyperexcitability remain unclear. To investigate interactions between endogenous tau and neuronal activity, we increased neuronal activity using bicuculline or suppressed activity using tetrodotoxin for 24 hours in rat hippocampal neurons. Chronic neuronal hyperactivity increased dendritic tau phosphorylation at S202/T205. This response was observed only in excitatory CA1 and CA3 pyramidal neurons, but not in inhibitory or dentate granule neurons. Dendritic phospho-tau positively correlated with neuronal activation after bicuculline treatment, as measured by cfos expression, and was prevented with inhibition of protein kinase A (PKA). To examine the functional consequences of tau S202/T205 phosphorylation, hippocampal neurons were transfected with wild-type tau or tau containing mutations preventing phosphorylation at these sites. Wildtype tau overexpression increased tau phosphorylation and reduced neuronal surface GluA2, which was prevented by phospho-site mutation. Extending these findings to mice, chronic low-dose picrotoxin treatment increased endogenous S202/T205 tau phosphorylation in CA3 neurons. In picrotoxin-treated mice, cfos expression was associated with increased tau phosphorylation specifically in CA3 and CA1 neurons. Together, these findings identify activity-dependent, endogenous tau phosphorylation as a selective response to hyperexcitation in excitatory hippocampal neurons downstream of PKA activation. The data further link tau phosphorylation to altered GluA2 regulation, suggesting a potential molecular connection between neuronal hyperactivity, tau phosphorylation, and synaptic dysfunction relevant to AD.

Comparative molecular mapping reveals molecular gradients and spatially organized populations in the subthalamic and para-subthalamic regions🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · Dumas, S., Francois, C., Biviano Rosenkilde, M. … Heuer, A., Karachi, C., Wallen-Mackenzie, A.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
The subthalamic nucleus (STN) is a major target for deep brain stimulation (DBS) in Parkinson disease (PD), yet its molecular organization and relationship to the adjacent para-subthalamic nucleus (pSTN) remain incompletely defined. Here, we performed comparative molecular mapping of the STN and pSTN in mouse and macaque, complemented by a human tissue sample, three-dimensional imaging, and publicly available spatial transcriptomic datasets. The STN showed a largely conserved molecular framework across species with gradients across rostrocaudal and dorsolateral axes. However, HTR2C was found enriched in the ventral STN of macaque and human tissue samples, while broadly distributed across the mouse STN. HTR2C showed opposite gradient of PVALB. In the mouse STN, Nts expression revealed an additional mediolateral gradient. Publicly available transcriptomic datasets showed concordant expression patterns. In both species, TAC1 characterized the canonical pSTN but also an extended population reaching dorsally along the medial aspect of the STN. TAC1-positive cells were thus found in the region traversed by tyrosine hydroxylase-positive fibers of the medial forebrain bundle (MFB). Together, these findings reveal a spatially heterogeneous, multiaxial molecular organization of the STN and a spatially complex molecular organization of its neighboring brain areas, refining the molecular and anatomical framework of the subthalamic region.

Perception of speech rate and intensity in Parkinson's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · DiNino, M., Heffner, C. C., Tjaden, K.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Purpose: Parkinson's disease (PD) is a neurodegenerative disease that affects motor control but can also influence sensory perception. Changes in vision and proprioception are well-documented but less is known about how PD alters auditory perception, particularly perception of speech acoustic properties. The current study examined perception of speech rate and intensity in PD and the relationship of auditory perception to disease severity. Method: People with PD were compared to age- and hearing-matched controls using perceptual tasks focused on discrimination and learning of speech rate and intensity. For rate discrimination, speech, non-speech, and visual stimuli were included to determine whether performance differences for PD participants and controls were specific to speech. Disease severity was assessed using the MDS-Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) and the relationship to performance on perceptual discrimination and learning tasks was evaluated. Results: People with PD performed significantly worse than controls in the rate discrimination task for all types of stimuli. There were no significant group differences for intensity discrimination. However, participants with greater PD disease severity demonstrated significantly poorer intensity discrimination accuracy. Performance on learning tasks utilizing rate and intensity manipulations did not differ between PD and control participants and was unrelated to PD disease severity. Conclusions: People with PD had difficulty discriminating rate differences across speech, non-speech, and visual stimuli, indicating that challenges with rate perception are not limited to speech. The relationship between intensity discrimination and disease severity may suggest common dopaminergic networks between motor symptoms and auditory perception in PD.

An EEG-fMRI Jointly Constrained Digital Twin Brain and Its Application in Alzheime's Disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-28 · Yue, X., Guo, D., Xu, Y. … Zeng, X., Guo, Y., Yao, D.（共 10 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Current digital twin brain (DTB) models are typically optimized using single-modality functional neuroimaging data, which restricts their ability to simulate brain dynamics across multiple spatiotemporal scales. Here, we show that a two-stage DTB (TS-DTB) modeling framework jointly constrained by functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) and electroencephalography (EEG) data. Validation in both the healthy young and Alzheimer's disease (AD) cohorts demonstrates that the TS-DTB model simultaneously captures subject-specific features of multi-scale brain dynamics. In particular, the TS-DTB models of AD patients successfully recapitulate spectral-temporal signatures of cognitive decline, suggesting a model-inferred association between these deficits and simulated excitation-inhibition (E-I) imbalances. By simulating responses to repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS), we further reveal that acute EEG normalization in AD patients is computationally associated with putative E-I rebalancing via model-inferred synaptic reconfiguration and background suppression. Overall, these findings underscore the potential of the TS-DTB to advance the mechanistic understanding of the brain and inform personalized digital therapeutics.
