A GPNMB-associated lysosomal program tracks neurodegeneration across the DAM continuum🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-20 · Werner, G., Schulte-Schrepping, J., Rodde, R. … Haass, C., Capell, A., Schultze（共 45 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract/title)
Frontotemporal dementia (FTD) and Alzheimer's disease (AD) profoundly remodel microglial states, yet how the heterogeneity of disease-associated microglia (DAM) relates to neurodegeneration remains incompletely understood. Using a progranulin (PGRN) and TMEM106B-deficient mouse model recapitulating key features of frontotemporal lobar degeneration (FTLD), including lysosomal impairment, we demonstrated that CSF1R inhibition reduced microgliosis yet accelerated neurodegeneration, suggesting a protective microglia contribution under lysosomal stress. Single-cell transcriptomics revealed that increasing Gpnmb expression within DAM was associated with a shift from immune and inflammatory processes toward a lysosomal, autolysosomal, and lipid-catabolic program. Across disease models, engagement of the GPNMB-associated program correlated with neurodegeneration. Consistently, A{beta}-driven models with limited neurodegeneration exhibited prominent DAM activation but comparatively limited GPNMB expression and engagement of this program. The GPNMB-associated program was TREM2-dependent, while loss of TREM2 signaling under lysosomal stress was associated with exacerbated neuronal damage. Conversely, lysosomal rescue markedly reduced the GPNMB-associated program, demonstrating its reversibility. CSF proteomics identified a convergent lysosomal and microglial signature across models overlapping with alterations reported in human FTD. Together, GPNMB marks reversible, TREM2-regulated lysosomal transcriptional adaptation along the DAM continuum that scales with lysosomal burden and neurodegenerative severity, consistent with a potentially protective microglial response under lysosomal stress.

Early locus coeruleus tauopathy features potassium channel dysfunction and is attenuated by Kv7 activation🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-20 · Zhang, Y.-C., Wang, Y.-G., Zhang, X.-T. … He, Z.-H., Xu, H.-T., He, K.-W.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract/title)
Background The locus coeruleus (LC) accumulates abnormal tau early in Alzheimer's disease, yet the mechanisms linking early tauopathy to neuronal dysfunction and the potential to modify subsequent pathology remain unclear. We investigated potassium-dependent excitability control, associated molecular 5 changes and Kv7-targeted intervention. Methods We combined electrophysiology, single-cell RNA sequencing, immunohistochemistry and sleep-electroencephalographic recordings in P301S tau transgenic (PS19) mice and wild-type mice receiving local LC inoculation of synthetic human tau fibrils. Pharmacological channel modulation and glycogen synthase kinase- 3 (GSK- 3) inhibition assessed pharmacological modulation of the electrophysiological phenotype. Chronic retigabine treatment assessed pathological outcomes after unilateral locus coeruleus fibril inoculation in young PS19 mice. Results Young, but not aged, PS19 mice exhibited elevated tonic firing and intrinsic excitability of locus 15 coeruleus neurons, associated with pre-tangle phosphorylated tau accumulation and reduced low-threshold potassium current density. Exploratory single-cell profiling identified candidate expression changes in marker-defined noradrenergic neurons: increased stress-associated gene expression coincided with reduced expression of neurofilament, neuronal-maintenance and potassium-channel genes, including Kcnq3 and Kcnd1. Increased nuclear RE-1 silencing 20 transcription factor (REST) and reduced Kv7.2 and Kv7.3 immunoreactivity suggested a candidate transcriptional mechanism associated with channel dysfunction. Local fibril inoculation reproduced the physiological abnormalities in wild-type mice, with hyperexcitability depending on endogenous tau. Combined Kv4/Kv7 modulation attenuated hyperexcitability, while GSK- 3; inhibition reduced tau phosphorylation, increased low-threshold potassium currents and lowered tonic firing. Both models showed increased dark-phase wakefulness and state-dependent electroencephalographic power changes. Chronic treatment with the Kv7 opener retigabine reduced bilateral LC AT8-positive tau burden and was associated with higher tyrosine hydroxylase (TH)-positive neuronal density after unilateral seeding. Conclusions Early LC tauopathy combines impaired potassium channel-dependent excitability control with transcriptional changes consistent with cellular stress and reduced neuronal maintenance. Kv7 activation attenuates seeded tau pathology, supporting excitability regulation as a candidate intervention strategy during early tauopathy.

Overexpression of α synuclein in Midbrain Dopamine Neurons Drives Learning Deficits in Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-20 · Coutant, B., Zhuang, X., Seiler, J. … Halliday, G., Lerner, T., Nelson, A. B.（共 10 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、alpha-synuclein』(命中abstract)
Parkinson disease (PD) is diagnosed based on core motor features. However, changes in cognitive function are often already present. Cognitive symptoms include changes in executive function, including organization, planning, multi-tasking, and flexible decision-making. These early cognitive deficits have been difficult to model and remain understudied. Here, we investigated whether AAV-based overexpression of wild-type human alpha-synuclein (syn) in midbrain dopaminergic neurons can produce deficits in learning and behavioral flexibility. We found that syn mice develop progressive impairment in basic action-outcome learning. In addition, we found that syn mice show impairments in behavioral flexibility during probabilistic reversal learning. Together, these findings demonstrate that syn overexpression in midbrain dopaminergic neurons is sufficient to drive cognitive decline. Our early-stage PD model offers a temporal window to dissect cellular and circuits mechanisms underlying non-motor manifestations of PD.

Microglia and microglia activation are protective in an LSD1-induced model of neurodegeneration🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-20 · Bai, Y., Gosrani, S. P., Han, C. … Engstrom, A. K., Soares, M. L., Katz, D. J.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『tau、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract/title)
Attempts to determine the function of microglia activation and the immune system in neurodegenerative diseases have yielded conflicting results, with some data showing a protective effect, while other data shows a detrimental effect. This discrepancy in the function of microglia activation and the immune system in neurodegeneration likely stems from differing functions during the build-up of pathological proteins versus later stage defects. Therefore, we investigated the function of microglia activation and the immune system in Lsd1 inducible deletion mice, which serve as a late-stage tau-associated neurodegeneration model without the build-up of pathological proteins. We found that loss of LSD1 causes the morphological and transcriptional activation of microglia, accompanied by astrocyte activation and a broader immune response. Blocking microglia activation by inactivating Trem2 inhibited the entire Lsd1-induced immune response, while pharmacologically inhibiting microglia via PLX3397 treatment eliminated microglia but did not block astrocyte reactivity or the broader immune response. Surprisingly, despite these effects, neither blocking microglia activation nor depleting microglia prevented Lsd1-induced neurodegeneration, paralysis and death. Instead, we found in both cases that survival was significantly reduced. These results suggest that microglia and microglial activation, independent of astrocyte reactivity and the broader inflammatory response, is neuroprotective in a late-stage tau-associated neurodegeneration model.

AAV-Mediated Astrogliosis Recapitulates Early Neuroinflammatory and Bioenergetic Features of Alzheimer''s Disease in Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Ramesh, A., Soni, N. D., Roy, D. … Detre, J., Reddy, R., Haris, M.（共 15 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration』(命中title/abstract)
Neuroinflammation and altered neuron-astrocyte metabolic interactions are increasingly recognized as early features of Alzheimer's disease (AD). However, the underlying metabolic adaptations remain incompletely understood. This study employed deuterium magnetic resonance spectroscopy (DMRS) following administration of deuterium labeled glucose and acetate to investigate neuronal and astroglial substrate utilization and metabolic fluxes, respectively, in adenovirus-induced neuroinflammation and early-stage APPNL-F/NL-F mice. In adenovirus-induced astrogliosis, increased acetate derived [4,4'-2H2]Glx (d2-Glx) production suggested enhanced astrocytic acetate utilization and TCA cycle activity. Conversely, reduced glucose derived d2-Glx despite comparable glucose uptake suggested a diminished neuronal activity due to potentially impaired neuronal oxidative metabolism. However, the glucose-derived lactate labeling remained unchanged, suggesting differential effects on oxidative and non-oxidative pathways of glucose metabolism. Similarly, 3-month-old APPNL-F/NL-F mice exhibited significantly increased [2,2',2''-2H3]acetate (d3-Ace) influx compared with wild-type controls, suggesting early alterations in astrocytic substrate utilization. While glucose uptake remained unchanged, glucose derived Glx and lactate showed modest, non-significant decreases, potentially reflecting early alterations in glucose metabolism. Overall, both models exhibited a metabolic pattern characterized by increased astrocytic acetate utilization with trends towards reduced glucose associated metabolism, with more pronounced changes in adenovirus-induced astrogliosis. These findings highlight the utility of DMRS for investigating dynamic metabolic adaptations during neuroinflammation and early AD pathogenesis. Importantly, the adenovirus-induced astrogliosis model recapitulates key metabolic alterations associated with the neuroinflammatory aspects of AD. As such, it provides a valuable complementary platform for investigating mechanisms driving early metabolic dysfunction and evaluating therapeutic strategies aimed at restoring neuron-astrocyte metabolic interactions prior to the onset of substantial neurodegeneration.

Preprocessing Determines Whether Fractal Biomarkers Detect Neurodegeneration: A Systematic Evaluation Across Five fMRI Pipelines🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Chattopadhyay, T., Cen, H. S., Thomopoulos, S. I. … Khopade, A., Gabitov, I., Thompson, P.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract/title)
Fractal analysis of resting-state functional MRI (rs-fMRI) has been proposed for detecting altered neural dynamics in Alzheimers disease (AD), but the effect of preprocessing on these measurements is poorly constrained. We evaluated 229 ADNI3 participants (156 cognitively normal [CN], 60 mild cognitive impairment [MCI], and 13 AD) processed with five ENIGMA HALFpipe variants. Each scan contained 197 volumes (TR = 3.0 s) and 434 regional time series. The reported CN-AD effect varied 4.5-fold for the detrended fluctuation analysis (DFA) Hurst exponent and 13.1-fold for Sample Entropy; both measures met p < 0.05 only after aCompCor. Under aCompCor, mean H was 0.812 in CN, 0.827 in MCI, and 0.872 in AD (d = -1.40, p = 0.0003), whereas the other four DFA effects were approximately d = -0.31 to -0.40. A variance decomposition attributed 41.5% of total H variance to pipeline, 44.5% to participant, and 0.3% to diagnosis. Whole-brain analysis found higher H in AD across 16 of 18 networks, with 36 cortical regions surviving false-discovery-rate correction. Synthetic fractional Gaussian noise experiments confirmed monotonic DFA estimation at the available scan length. The small AD group makes all disease-effect estimates exploratory. These results show that preprocessing choice can determine whether fractal group differences are detectable and should therefore be treated as part of the biomarker definition.

Prolonged loss of D1 receptor signaling promotes maladaptive plasticity of direct pathway striatal projection neurons🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Martin, J. R., Power, S. K., Cavanagh, A. E. … Dasen, J. S., Kang, U. J., Tritsch, N. X.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
As Parkinson's disease (PD) progresses and nigrostriatal dopamine (DA) neuron degeneration worsens, DA replacement therapies shift from restoring motor function to evoking dyskinesia. How disease progression mediates this transition remains poorly understood. Here, we optimized a 6-hydroxydopamine (6OHDA) model that reliably generates multiple severities of DA axon denervation within the striatum of individual mice. We identify a critical denervation threshold at which direct-pathway striatal projection neurons (dSPNs), which are key drivers of dyskinesia become pathologically hyperresponsive to DA. Using slice electrophysiology, we establish that denervation-induced increase in intrinsic excitability is insufficient to explain the aberrant response of dSPNs to DA. We further demonstrate using genetic and chemogenetic manipulations that prolonged loss of D1 receptor signaling drives the pathological transformation of dSPNs cell-autonomously, and that sustained Gs-coupled metabotropic receptor signaling in dSPNs is sufficient to both prevent and reverse it. These findings provide a mechanistic framework for understanding how PD progression renders striatal circuits vulnerable to DA-evoked motor complications and identify sustained signaling by Gs-coupled receptors as a potential avenue for preventing or reversing denervation-induced maladaptive striatal plasticity.

Probabilistic mouse-human brain map translation through multimodal optimal transport🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Koch, S. P., Perales, M., Sil, T. … Reich, M., Boehm-Sturm, P., Peach, R. L.（共 17 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、alpha-synuclein』(命中abstract)
Cross-species differences in brain organisation complicate the interpretation of mouse experiments and the selection of mouse regions from human imaging findings. Here we present OTTER, which combines functional and structural connectivity, spatial position and comparative anatomy through optimal transport to estimate probabilistic mouse-human brain correspondence. The mapping recovers established regional relationships and supports bidirectional translation of experimental, functional and molecular maps. Assessment of the connectomes used to fit OTTER shows lower mouse-based reconstruction accuracy for human functional connectivity in the expanded association cortex compared to sensory and motor regions. Cross-sectional human Parkinson disease maps yield translated cortical thinning scores that increase across stages in the mouse visceral, gustatory and agranular-insular regions relative to the motor cortex. The corresponding anatomical contrast increases over time in a separate longitudinal MRI analysis following alpha-synuclein fibril injection in mice. OTTER predicts human maps from mouse experiments and candidate mouse targets from human imaging.

Lipidome of Compact Myelin from Cortical Regions of the Aging Murine Brain🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Liu, V. P., Johnson, C. E., Lakhani, S., Furber, K. L.
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Age-related cognitive decline has been associated with neurodegeneration in cortical white matter tracts. The trajectory of white matter degeneration over the lifespan follows a similar pattern in humans, primates and rodents including the loss of myelin integrity and concomitant loss in myelinated axonal segments. Previous studies suggest that biochemical alterations in myelin begin to occur well prior to myelin loss. To better understand these biochemical determinants underlying white matter degeneration, we investigated the lipid composition of myelin isolated from young adult, middle-aged and aged murine brain. Compact myelin was enriched from cortical regions using a refined density centrifugation protocol and profiled the lipidome using LC-MS/MS. Over 2000 lipid species were identified, with the relative expression of 414 species changing over the age-span. The myelin lipidome from young mice was biochemically distinct compared to the aging cohorts. Aging was characterized by an overall decrease in high abundant species of glycerophospholipid and sphingolipids, including phosphocholine, phosphoethanolamine, sphingomyelin and cerebrosides. Pathway analysis by LipidSig 2.0 and lipid ontology analysis by LION/web highlighted alterations to lipid metabolism and membrane biophysical properties related to bilayer stability and organization. These data support the concept that myelin lipid compositional changes are apparent by middle age which underly structural changes in myelin at later ages. The findings provide greater insight into age-related biochemical alterations in the myelin sheath and may aid in strategies to mitigate white matter degeneration over the lifespan.

Evolutionarily conserved coupling of lysosomal response sensing and disease-associated microglial reprogramming scales with pathological timescales🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Liang, Y., Yuan, H., Zhao, Y. … Wang, J., Liyi, L., Zhang, Y.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Background Microglia transition into disease-associated microglia (DAM) during central nervous system (CNS) injury and neurodegeneration, yet the upstream intracellular logic driving this state conversion in vivo remains unclear. The lysosome acts as a signaling hub, but whether physiological phagocytic load universally initiates and quantitatively couples to DAM reprogramming across different pathological timescales is unknown. Methods We established a rigorous, unit-corrected analytical framework to overcome pseudo-replication in public single-cell data, anchoring statistical inferences at the level of independent biological units (animals, sequencing plates, or donors). We integrated six transcriptomic datasets spanning three murine injury/amyloidosis models and three large-scale human neurodegenerative cohorts (>320,000 microglial nuclei), alongside human Alzheimer's disease (AD) GWAS summary statistics. Results We demonstrate that the MiT/TFE-regulated lysosomal biogenesis program temporally precedes DAM reprogramming and maintains an evolutionarily conserved baseline coupling even in uninjured homeostatic tissue. In human microglia, MITF and TFE3 transcripts closely track DAM activation, whereas post-translationally regulated TFEB mRNA does not. Genetic enrichment analysis (MAGMA) reveals that common AD risk does not act upon the upstream lysosomal program, but is strongly anchored downstream at APOE. Furthermore, Trem2-knockout models demonstrate that disease-induced coupling amplification retains roughly half its magnitude even when the mature DAM subpopulation collapses. Cross-system comparisons reveal a state-transition synchrony model: disease-driven amplification of lysosomal-DAM coupling ({Delta}) scales with pathological timescales, showing strong amplification in acute murine injury and aggressive amyloidosis (5xFAD {Delta} = +0.252), but manifesting primarily as steady baseline maintenance across decades-long asynchronous human AD progression (SEA-AD {Delta} = +0.072). Conclusions Our study establishes the lysosomal stress-DAM coupling as an evolutionarily conserved pacemaking module for microglial state transitions, providing a quantitative framework for therapeutically targeting neuroinflammation and tissue repair.
