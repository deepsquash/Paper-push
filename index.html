Chronic evoked seizures differentially worsen cognitive function and long-term neuropsychiatric comorbidities in Alzheimer's disease-related genotypes🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Knox, K., Fang, J., Davidson, S., Barker-Haliski, M.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Variants in one of three deterministic risk genes - amyloid precursor protein (APP), presenilin 1 (PSEN1), or presenilin 2 (PSEN2) - lead to early-onset Alzheimer's disease (EOAD), which also carries a high risk of spontaneous seizures. Seizures may worsen AD-related behavioral comorbidities, including depression, anxiety, and cognitive impairment. Using the corneal kindling model of secondarily generalized focal seizures in two distinct aged male and female EOAD-associated mouse models, we assessed the interaction between investigator-initiated chronic seizures and EOAD genotype on AD-related behavioral comorbidities. Genotypes were selected to model amyloid-b; independent and dependent AD risk factors and included: PSEN2-N141I (amyloid-b; independent model) versus wild-type (PSEN2 control) mice, and APP/PS1 (amyloid-b dependent model) versus wild-type littermates (Tg-). Sham and fully kindled mice then underwent behavioral assessment in the saccharin preference test of anhedonia-like behavior, the Barnes maze test of spatial learning and memory, and a 67-hour home cage monitoring of circadian activity. EOAD genotype only significantly affected kindling acquisition of aged APP/PS1 females. However, there was no significant genotype or kindling effect on saccharin preference, suggesting no adverse impact on anhedonia-like behavior. Cognitive performance in the Barnes maze was significantly worsened by seizures exclusively in APP/PS1 female mice. However, seizures generally worsened Barnes maze performance of male PSEN2-N141I and PSEN2 control mice. Finally, chronic seizures consistently worsened exploratory behavior patterns during home cage circadian activity monitoring exclusively in APP/PS1 male and female mice. This study reveals the AD-related heterogeneity of seizure-evoked behavioral consequences on spatial working memory and circadian behaviors.

Identification of a human Anxa1-associated dopaminergic population linked to Parkinson's disease vulnerability and sensorimotor putaminal networks🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Schonfeld, E., Baliki, M. N., Monje, M. H. G. … Surmeier, D. J., Dombeck, D. A., Awatramani, R.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
The selective loss of dopaminergic (DA) neurons drives motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease (PD). In mice, Anxa1+ SNc DA neurons are highly vulnerable in PD models, project to the dorsolateral striatum, and display movement-correlated activity, yet they lack clearly defined human counterparts. We transfer the mouse taxonomy to human using a hierarchical mixture-of-experts model, recovering a human Anxa1-associated population. We find that this was the most depleted subtype in PD brains. A ligand-receptor algorithm, validated against mouse tracing, infers that these neurons preferentially innervate the caudal putamen. There, DaT-SPECT shows the earliest and most severe DA axon loss in PD. Functional connectivity positions this region as a critical node within the somato-cognitive action network, linking DA neuron vulnerability to circuit dysfunction. To translate these insights, we present an interactive portal unifying human datasets with a cross-species FDA Drug Atlas, nominating CACNA1G as a candidate neuroprotective target for the Anxa1-associated population.

Modulation of genetic- and toxicant-induced Parkinson's disease outcomes by SV2C in C. elegans🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Knickerbocker, A. C., Chung, H., Miller, G. W., Bucher, M. L.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Several proteins involved in dopaminergic neurotransmission have been implicated in Parkinson's disease (PD), including synaptic vesicle glycoprotein 2C (SV2C). At least seven SNPs in SV2C are associated with PD, including variants that modify: PD risk, PD risk in GBA carriers, PD severity, contribution of environmental factors (e.g., nicotine) to PD, and PD patient response to L-DOPA and dopamine therapies. Although the specific function of SV2C remains unknown, there is significant evidence supporting a role of SV2C in mediating vesicular dopamine content, vesicular trafficking, and neurotransmitter release. Furthermore, SV2C directly associates with -synuclein and mediates the content of neurotoxicants within synaptic vesicles. Taken together, these data suggest that SV2C plays a critical role in mediating PD risk and pathogenesis. To investigate whether SV2C mediates PD outcomes, transgenic Caenorhabditis elegans (C. elegans) expressing human SV2C, human -synuclein, or both, were exposed to the PD-associated neurotoxicants 1-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium (MPP+), rotenone, and paraquat, and evaluated for PD outcomes including lethality and dopamine-mediated motor behavior. Although SV2C-expressing C. elegans showed a shorter lifespan at baseline, SV2C expression conferred organism-level protection against MPP+ and paraquat-induced lethality. While -synuclein expression exacerbated lethality following MPP+ and paraquat exposure, SV2C provided partial rescue of lethality in -synuclein-expressing C. elegans. However, no differences in lethality were observed based on genotype following rotenone exposure. Interestingly, sublethal toxicant exposure did not reveal a protective effect of SV2C expression on motor behavior for any of the toxicants. To determine whether SV2C's modulation of vesicular uptake mediated the protection against lethality from PD-associated neurotoxicants observed in C. elegans, in vitro assays of vesicular dopamine uptake dynamics were performed. SV2C expression resulted in a protective shift in toxicant-induced uptake inhibition following MPP+ and rotenone exposure; however, there was no paraquat-induced inhibition of vesicular uptake observed in vitro. Taken together, these data suggest that while SV2C plays a protective role in mediating organism-level survival in C. elegans following MPP+ and paraquat exposure, the mechanisms of protection and the role of SV2C in modulating vesicular dopamine dynamics and dopamine-mediated behaviors following toxicant exposure require further analysis. Future studies can evaluate additional PD outcomes in these exposure models and investigate SV2C-mediated mechanisms of protection such as regulation of neurotransmitter handling and oxidative stress.

Glial involvement in mitochondrial metabolic alterations in Alzheimer's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Hirata, K., Ono, M., Maeda, Y. … Raj, T., Uchida, Y., Takado, Y.（共 17 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中title)
Metabolic alterations in the posterior cingulate cortex (PCC) emerge early in Alzheimer's disease (AD), but their molecular and cellular basis remains unclear. We hypothesized that impaired glial energy metabolism, particularly mitochondrial oxidative metabolism in astrocytes, underlies the metabolic vulnerability of the PCC in AD. To test this hypothesis, we integrated human PCC proteomics and transcriptomics with magnetic resonance spectroscopy in AppNL-G-F knock-in (APP-KI) mice. Human proteomic and transcriptomic analyses converged on reduced mitochondrial oxidative-metabolism signatures associated with AD pathology and cognition. APP-KI hippocampus showed proteomic and 1H-MRS alterations resembling those in the human AD PCC. Spatial transcriptomics identified widespread metabolic transcriptional changes and reduced mitochondrial pathway signatures in glia-enriched hippocampal clusters. Hyperpolarized [1-13C]pyruvate spectroscopy showed reduced bicarbonate labelling in APP-KI mice, consistent with impaired oxidative pyruvate metabolism. These findings reveal convergent mitochondrial alterations in human AD and APP-KI mice, implicating glial cells, particularly astrocytes, in the associated dysfunction.

Lipidomic Profiling of L1CAM+ Extracellular Vesicles in Parkinson's Disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Abdulsalam, M. A., Eylem, C. C., Avci, I. … Caglayan, M. G., Yilmaz, R., Derkus, B.（共 15 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Parkinson's disease (PD) lacks minimally invasive biomarkers that reflect neuron-derived molecular alterations. Here, we performed a pilot study for untargeted Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS)-based lipidomics on serum total extracellular vesicles (tEVs) and L1CAM-enriched EVs (L1CAM+EVs) in patients with PD and healthy controls (n=10 per group; N=40). Partial least squares-discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) separated PD from control samples in both fractions L1CAM+EVs: R2Y = 0.998, Q2 = 0.879; tEVs: R2Y = 0.998, Q2 = 0.876), and distinguished L1CAM+EVs from tEVs within PD samples (R2Y = 0.999, Q2 = 0.925). In PD tEVs, triacylglycerols, fatty acids, phosphatidylcholines, sphingomyelins and phosphatidylserines were increased, and pathway analysis indicated enhanced conversion of phosphatidylcholine (PC) and phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) to phosphatidylserine (PS). In contrast, PD L1CAM+EVs showed increased DG, LPA and PS with reduced LPC; pathway reconstruction indicated enhanced PE-PC-DG and LPC-PC flux with reduced de novo PC and SM synthesis. Within PD, L1CAM+EVs were depleted of multiple sphingomyelin species (e.g., SM 41:2;O3, SM 35:2;O3, SM 41:3;O3) and glycerophospholipids relative to tEVs, whereas SPB 18:0;O2 and FA 16:4 were relatively enriched. These findings indicate that L1CAM-enriched EVs carry a PD-associated lipid signature that is qualitatively distinct from that of controls, supporting their evaluation as a complementary source of neuron-related lipid biomarkers.

The adaptor protein DAB2 cell-autonomously links APOE with disease-associated microglia (DAM)/TREM2 state dysregulation associated with Alzheimer's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Salih, D. A., Barber, M., Andrews, W. D. … Howell, B. W., Parnavelas, J., Hardy, J.（共 22 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
The neuron-secreted Reelin (RELN) protein and its adaptor DAB1 modify Alzheimer's disease (AD) risk through genetic interaction with APOE4. While RELN and APOE4 exert antagonistic effects on amyloid and Tau pathology in mice, how risk is conferred in the human brain remains unclear. The related adaptor DAB2 endocytoses RELN/APOE receptors and, via LRP8, modulates non-canonical pro-inflammatory signalling in non-neuronal cells. We hypothesised that RELN and APOE signalling directly influence microglial function to modify AD risk. Integrating human brain transcriptomics, a Reln-deficient mouse, and DAB1/DAB2 knock-down in APOE isogenic iPSC-microglia, we found that microglial DAB1/DAB2 pathways were dynamically regulated across the lifespan, with DAB2 dysregulated with increasing amyloid pathology. DAB2 transcriptional networks correlated with disease- and lipid- associated microglial states (DAM/LDAM) linked to networks expressing dementia-risk loci (TREM2, GRN, ACE, SORL1) and associated with endolysosomal and lipid-handling pathways. DAB2- knockdown cell-autonomously shifted microglia away from gene networks enriched in DAM/TREM2 states and towards pro-inflammatory cytokine-responsive states and TNFNF{kappa}B pathways. APOE4/E4 microglia showed elevated APOE and reduced DAB1pY232 under inflammatory stress, and blunted DAB2-knockdown transcriptomic responses, consistent with pathway-level genetic interaction. In vivo, RELN-deficiency reduced microglial ramification and increased CD68. These findings identify DAB2 as a regulator of microglial state transitions and a modifier of dementia-risk networks linking APOE genotype to inflammatory, phagocytic and amyloid-beta clearance pathways relevant to AD.

MK-6240🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-23 · Biju, A. P., Herrera, J. M., McNamara, C. C., Hipolito, E. L. M., Liang, C., Mukherjee, J.
关键词『Alzheimer、Parkinson、tau』(命中abstract)
Flortaucipir and MK-6240 are two tau imaging agents currently in use for measuring neurofibrillary tangle (NFT) progression in Alzheimers disease (AD). The aims of this study were to use postmortem AD and Down syndrome (DS) brain sections to study three issues: (1) Measure binding affinity of flortaucipir and MK-6240 in AD brain sections by using [125I]IPPI; (2) Evaluate the binding characteristics of [18F]flortaucipir in AD, DS and cognitively normal (CN) brains sections and compare them with [18F]MK-6240, and (3) Assess the binding of [18F]flortaucipir in CN and Parkinsons disease (PD) brain sections to identify any off-target binding. The half-maximal inhibition (IC50) of [125I]IPPI bound to anterior cingulate (AC) in AD brain sections for flortaucipir was 2.91 nM and MK-6240 was 5.99 nM. Using frontal cortex (FCX) and temporal cortex (TCX) of AD, DS and CN brain sections, [18F]flortaucipir displayed high levels of binding in the gray matter (GM) of AD and DS. The average ratio of DSAD GM to CN GM was 15.7 in FCX, while DSAD GM to CN GM was 15.2 for the TCX. In the case of AD, average ratio of AD GM to CN GM was 12.4 in FCX, while AD GM to CN GM was 9.5 for the TCX. Binding of [18F]flortaucipir in brain section of AC from CN and PD cases did not exhibit any off-target binding to monoamine oxidase-A (MAO-A). In summary, both flortaucipir and MK-6240 exhibit similar high affinity to tau and [18F]flortaucipir exhibits higher binding compared to [18F]MK-6240 in DSAD.

A latent tau amplifier in the neuronal proteome lowers the seeding threshold for Alzheimers disease pathology🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-22 · Tsaka, G., Kolla, R., Wiedmer, A. E. … Thal, D. R., Rousseau, F., Schymkowitz, J.（共 19 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中title)
The stereotyped spread of tau pathology through connected brain regions is accompanied by striking differences in the vulnerability of individual neuronal populations, pointing to cell-intrinsic determinants of susceptibility. Using a combination of computational APR analysis, in vitro and cellular aggregation assays, and quantitative proteomics of human postmortem brain, we identify the splicing factor TRA2B as an endogenous modifier of this susceptibility. TRA2B enhances the activity of patient-derived tau seeds, promotes tau pathology in vivo, and progressively redistributes from the nucleus into insoluble tau-associated material in Alzheimers disease brain. Mechanistically, TRA2B contains two aggregation-prone regions (APRs) homologous to tau nucleation sites that are required for tau interaction and seeding enhancement. Yet this activity is not constitutive: isolated TRA2B APRs promote aggregation, whereas the full-length protein can inhibit, amplify or have little effect on tau aggregation depending on protein state and seeding context. Perturbation of the regulatory RS2 region switches TRA2B away from its amplifying state, revealing that its sequence-encoded aggregation potential is regulatable. Thus, TRA2B acts as a latent tau amplifier, providing a mechanism by which the neuronal proteome can tune cellular susceptibility to the propagation of pathological tau.

The exocyst is an insulin-sensitive regulator of amyloid precursor protein trafficking and amyloid-beta generation in neurons🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-22 · Balaan, C., Patwardhan, G. Y., Sachs, R. K. … Ortega, M. A., Nichols, R. A., Fogelgren, B.（共 14 位作者）
关键词『amyloid』(命中title)
Intracellular trafficking of amyloid precursor protein (APP) critically influences amyloidogenic processing, yet the mechanisms regulating this pathway remain incompletely defined. The exocyst is a highly conserved, insulin-responsive, eight-protein Rab effector complex that directs intracellular transport vesicle targeting and docking. We identified APP in a proteomics screen of neuronal cell surface proteins altered following chemical inhibition of exocyst activity. In SH-SY5Y cells expressing an APP mutant that enhances amyloidogenic processing, RNAi-mediated silencing of exocyst subunits significantly decreased sAPP and A{beta} secretion, accompanied by intracellular APP accumulation. High-resolution microscopy revealed APP colocalization with exocyst subunits in the soma and neurites of differentiated SH-SY5Y neurons and primary mouse hippocampal neurons. Live-cell TIRF microscopy demonstrated coordinated movement of fluorescently tagged exocyst and APP proteins. Proximity ligation assays (PLAs) confirmed close (<40nm) APP-EXOC5 association in differentiated SH-SY5Y neurons, primary mouse hippocampal neurons, and histological sections of mouse brain. To examine whether insulin regulates neuronal exocyst activity, as in adipocytes and muscle, we generated SH-SY5Y cells expressing pHluorin-tagged GLUT4. Using this model, we found that chemical inhibition of exocyst activity prevented insulin-stimulated GLUT4 exocytosis to the plasma membrane. Furthermore, PLAs in both primary hippocampal and SH-SY5Y neurons revealed that insulin increased GLUT4-EXOC5 associations while markedly reducing APP-EXOC5 associations. These findings suggest that insulin signaling redirects the exocyst away from APP-positive vesicles towards GLUT-positive vesicles. Together, our data identify the exocyst as a novel insulin-regulated mediator of neuronal APP trafficking and A{beta} secretion.
