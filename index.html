Multi-parametric spectral modeling disentangles synaptic kinetics and neural excitability from non-invasive electrophysiological recordings🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-18 · Philippe, V., Tombroff, L., Akbarian, F. … Strauss, M., De Tiege, X., Wens, V.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Spectral modeling of electrophysiological recordings enables non-invasive characterization of neural excitability. However, current approaches rely on a single spectral index, limiting insight into the multiple biophysical processes underlying excitation-inhibition balance. Here, we introduce a biologically informed, multi-parametric spectral analysis that disentangles neural excitability and synaptic kinetics from recordings. As a proof of concept, we demonstrate that this approach captures signatures of hyperexcitability and glutamate reuptake dysfunction characteristic of Alzheimer's disease.

Spatiotemporal transcriptomic dynamic across the auditory pathway from young to old age in 5xFAD Alzheimer disease mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-18 · Kong, Y., Zhai, T.-Y., Zhao, H.-B.
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中title)
Auditory dysfunction can precede clinical dementia, but the molecular progression of Alzheimer disease (AD) pathology from the sensory periphery to auditory cortex remains poorly resolved. We performed bulk RNA sequencing of auditory cortex (AC), inferior colliculus (IC), cochlear nucleus (CN), and cochlea from 5xFAD and wild-type mice at postnatal day 50 (P50), 8 months, 11 months, and 16 months. Age-specific genotype effects and Genotype x Age interactions were evaluated using DESeq2, followed by Hallmark, KEGG, and Gene Ontology gene-set enrichment analyses and a cross-region synthesis of interaction trajectories. At P50, AC, IC, and cochlea showed coordinated suppression of mitochondrial metabolism, translation, RNA processing, and proteostasis, whereas CN displayed the opposite metabolic and biosynthetic profile together with reduced synaptic and adhesion programs. At 8 months, inflammatory interactions converged across all four regions, including interferon, complement, IL6-JAK-STAT3, and broader immune-response pathways. This coordination fractured at 11 months: AC and IC retained inflammatory and negative synaptic shifts, whereas CN and cochlea frequently changed in the opposite direction, including transient positive shifts in synaptic programs. By 16 months, immune interactions converged again, while metabolic, proteostatic, and synaptic trajectories remained region dependent; CN opposed AC, IC, and cochlea in several major metabolic programs. These findings define a distributed, asynchronous response rather than a monotonic bottom-up or top-down progression. The resulting spatiotemporal map links early metabolic stress, intermediate central-peripheral decoupling, and late inflammatory convergence, providing a molecular framework for evaluating peripheral to central auditory gain and temporal-processing measures as candidate indicators of AD progression.

Cognitive tests of pattern separation as sleep-sensitive measures in middle-aged and older adults: An exploratory study🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-17 · Roenningen, A. E., Gill, D., Kent, B. A.
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Sleep disturbances are considered both a dementia risk factor and symptom. The present research aimed to identify cognitive tests sensitive to objective sleep quantity or quality, focusing on tests designed to evaluate the earliest cognitive changes in dementia. We recruited adults who were 50 years or older and remotely monitored their sleep patterns for 7 consecutive days using wrist actigraphy and sleep diaries. On day 7, participants completed different cognitive tests; the Psychomotor Vigilance Task (PVT), the Prodromal Alzheimers and Mild Cognitive Impairment battery from the Cambridge Neuropsychological Test Automated Battery (CANTAB), the Mnemonic Similarity Task (MST), taxing pattern separation, and the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA). The final sample included 34 participants (mean age: 65.56, SD: 9.57). There were significant correlations between objective sleep quantity and PVT and MST performance. MoCA scores were correlated with performance on CANTAB and MST. Objective sleep quantity predicted MST performance when controlling for age, sex, and education. Tests of pattern separation are sensitive to objective sleep quantity and early cognitive changes associated with dementia. The sensitivity of pattern separation tests to sleep should be evaluated in a larger study, aiming to identify suitable clinical trial outcome measures for sleep-promoting treatments in older adults.

Brainstem cholinergic cell loss causes slowly-developing motor impairments🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-17 · Udobi, K., Aleman, V., Lewitus, V. … Forcelli, P. A., Neckel, N., Evans, R. C.（共 12 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Pedunculopontine nucleus (PPN) cholinergic degeneration is a feature of Parkinson's disease (PD), but it is difficult to isolate the symptoms and circuit alterations caused by cholinergic cell loss in PD, where multiple nuclei are simultaneously degenerating. Here we develop a novel mouse model of brainstem cholinergic cell loss to define the timeline of symptom development and map physiological adaptations within the brainstem. Mild motor symptoms, including balance and motor learning deficits reminiscent of early-stage PD, developed after cholinergic lesion with an unexpected long delay. Ex vivo whole-cell recordings showed that cholinergic cell loss increased excitatory synaptic drive onto glutamatergic PPN neurons and increased their intrinsic ability to generate sustained action potential output. In parallel, fiber photometry experiments revealed a slight increase in glutamatergic sensory responsiveness that closely tracked the delayed onset of behavioral symptoms. Together, these results reveal that brainstem cholinergic cell loss triggers the reorganization of motor-related brainstem circuits, providing critical insight into the consequences of non-dopaminergic degeneration in PD.

NeuroMark dFNC: Transferable Dynamic-State Templates for Subject-Specific and Frequency-Resolved Chronnectomics🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-17 · Esfahani, M. M., Seraji, M., Salem, S. … Adali, T., Wang, Y.-P., Calhoun, V.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Dynamic functional network connectivity (dFNC) commonly identifies recurring connectivity states from resting-state fMRI, but states derived independently in each dataset lack guaranteed correspondence across studies and cannot be directly estimated in new participants. We introduce NeuroMark dFNC, which extends the NeuroMark principle of prior-guided network estimation to dynamic connectivity by using reproducible reference states as priors for estimating corresponding subject-specific states. Reference-state templates were derived from UK Biobank using windowed functional network connectivity and time-frequency moments (TFMs), with split-sample reproducibility evaluated for k-means, independent component analysis (ICA), and principal component analysis (PCA) template-generation approaches. Constrained ICA then used the templates to estimate subject-specific state patterns and continuous loadings in independent FBIRN schizophrenia and ADNI Alzheimer's disease cohorts. ICA loadings were calibrated by their contribution to reconstruction of the original feature matrix. Fractional occupancy and conditional mean dwell time were calculated from the state with the largest absolute calibrated loading at each observation. The predefined templates avoided the need for dataset-specific state discovery and post-hoc state matching. In both clinical cohorts, the analysis identified state-dependent, distributed edgewise differences and differences in selected temporal summaries. Nominal frequency-resolved findings varied across states and moment-frequency bins and were treated as exploratory. NeuroMark dFNC provides a common state space for cross-cohort comparison while retaining subject-specific connectivity patterns and continuous state expression.
