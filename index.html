AAV-Mediated Astrogliosis Recapitulates Early Neuroinflammatory and Bioenergetic Features of Alzheimer''s Disease in Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Ramesh, A., Soni, N. D., Roy, D. … Detre, J., Reddy, R., Haris, M.（共 15 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration』(命中title/abstract)
Neuroinflammation and altered neuron-astrocyte metabolic interactions are increasingly recognized as early features of Alzheimer's disease (AD). However, the underlying metabolic adaptations remain incompletely understood. This study employed deuterium magnetic resonance spectroscopy (DMRS) following administration of deuterium labeled glucose and acetate to investigate neuronal and astroglial substrate utilization and metabolic fluxes, respectively, in adenovirus-induced neuroinflammation and early-stage APPNL-F/NL-F mice. In adenovirus-induced astrogliosis, increased acetate derived [4,4'-2H2]Glx (d2-Glx) production suggested enhanced astrocytic acetate utilization and TCA cycle activity. Conversely, reduced glucose derived d2-Glx despite comparable glucose uptake suggested a diminished neuronal activity due to potentially impaired neuronal oxidative metabolism. However, the glucose-derived lactate labeling remained unchanged, suggesting differential effects on oxidative and non-oxidative pathways of glucose metabolism. Similarly, 3-month-old APPNL-F/NL-F mice exhibited significantly increased [2,2',2''-2H3]acetate (d3-Ace) influx compared with wild-type controls, suggesting early alterations in astrocytic substrate utilization. While glucose uptake remained unchanged, glucose derived Glx and lactate showed modest, non-significant decreases, potentially reflecting early alterations in glucose metabolism. Overall, both models exhibited a metabolic pattern characterized by increased astrocytic acetate utilization with trends towards reduced glucose associated metabolism, with more pronounced changes in adenovirus-induced astrogliosis. These findings highlight the utility of DMRS for investigating dynamic metabolic adaptations during neuroinflammation and early AD pathogenesis. Importantly, the adenovirus-induced astrogliosis model recapitulates key metabolic alterations associated with the neuroinflammatory aspects of AD. As such, it provides a valuable complementary platform for investigating mechanisms driving early metabolic dysfunction and evaluating therapeutic strategies aimed at restoring neuron-astrocyte metabolic interactions prior to the onset of substantial neurodegeneration.

Preprocessing Determines Whether Fractal Biomarkers Detect Neurodegeneration: A Systematic Evaluation Across Five fMRI Pipelines🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Chattopadhyay, T., Cen, H. S., Thomopoulos, S. I. … Khopade, A., Gabitov, I., Thompson, P.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract/title)
Fractal analysis of resting-state functional MRI (rs-fMRI) has been proposed for detecting altered neural dynamics in Alzheimers disease (AD), but the effect of preprocessing on these measurements is poorly constrained. We evaluated 229 ADNI3 participants (156 cognitively normal [CN], 60 mild cognitive impairment [MCI], and 13 AD) processed with five ENIGMA HALFpipe variants. Each scan contained 197 volumes (TR = 3.0 s) and 434 regional time series. The reported CN-AD effect varied 4.5-fold for the detrended fluctuation analysis (DFA) Hurst exponent and 13.1-fold for Sample Entropy; both measures met p < 0.05 only after aCompCor. Under aCompCor, mean H was 0.812 in CN, 0.827 in MCI, and 0.872 in AD (d = -1.40, p = 0.0003), whereas the other four DFA effects were approximately d = -0.31 to -0.40. A variance decomposition attributed 41.5% of total H variance to pipeline, 44.5% to participant, and 0.3% to diagnosis. Whole-brain analysis found higher H in AD across 16 of 18 networks, with 36 cortical regions surviving false-discovery-rate correction. Synthetic fractional Gaussian noise experiments confirmed monotonic DFA estimation at the available scan length. The small AD group makes all disease-effect estimates exploratory. These results show that preprocessing choice can determine whether fractal group differences are detectable and should therefore be treated as part of the biomarker definition.

Prolonged loss of D1 receptor signaling promotes maladaptive plasticity of direct pathway striatal projection neurons🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Martin, J. R., Power, S. K., Cavanagh, A. E. … Dasen, J. S., Kang, U. J., Tritsch, N. X.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
As Parkinson's disease (PD) progresses and nigrostriatal dopamine (DA) neuron degeneration worsens, DA replacement therapies shift from restoring motor function to evoking dyskinesia. How disease progression mediates this transition remains poorly understood. Here, we optimized a 6-hydroxydopamine (6OHDA) model that reliably generates multiple severities of DA axon denervation within the striatum of individual mice. We identify a critical denervation threshold at which direct-pathway striatal projection neurons (dSPNs), which are key drivers of dyskinesia become pathologically hyperresponsive to DA. Using slice electrophysiology, we establish that denervation-induced increase in intrinsic excitability is insufficient to explain the aberrant response of dSPNs to DA. We further demonstrate using genetic and chemogenetic manipulations that prolonged loss of D1 receptor signaling drives the pathological transformation of dSPNs cell-autonomously, and that sustained Gs-coupled metabotropic receptor signaling in dSPNs is sufficient to both prevent and reverse it. These findings provide a mechanistic framework for understanding how PD progression renders striatal circuits vulnerable to DA-evoked motor complications and identify sustained signaling by Gs-coupled receptors as a potential avenue for preventing or reversing denervation-induced maladaptive striatal plasticity.

Probabilistic mouse-human brain map translation through multimodal optimal transport🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Koch, S. P., Perales, M., Sil, T. … Reich, M., Boehm-Sturm, P., Peach, R. L.（共 17 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、alpha-synuclein』(命中abstract)
Cross-species differences in brain organisation complicate the interpretation of mouse experiments and the selection of mouse regions from human imaging findings. Here we present OTTER, which combines functional and structural connectivity, spatial position and comparative anatomy through optimal transport to estimate probabilistic mouse-human brain correspondence. The mapping recovers established regional relationships and supports bidirectional translation of experimental, functional and molecular maps. Assessment of the connectomes used to fit OTTER shows lower mouse-based reconstruction accuracy for human functional connectivity in the expanded association cortex compared to sensory and motor regions. Cross-sectional human Parkinson disease maps yield translated cortical thinning scores that increase across stages in the mouse visceral, gustatory and agranular-insular regions relative to the motor cortex. The corresponding anatomical contrast increases over time in a separate longitudinal MRI analysis following alpha-synuclein fibril injection in mice. OTTER predicts human maps from mouse experiments and candidate mouse targets from human imaging.

Lipidome of Compact Myelin from Cortical Regions of the Aging Murine Brain🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Liu, V. P., Johnson, C. E., Lakhani, S., Furber, K. L.
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Age-related cognitive decline has been associated with neurodegeneration in cortical white matter tracts. The trajectory of white matter degeneration over the lifespan follows a similar pattern in humans, primates and rodents including the loss of myelin integrity and concomitant loss in myelinated axonal segments. Previous studies suggest that biochemical alterations in myelin begin to occur well prior to myelin loss. To better understand these biochemical determinants underlying white matter degeneration, we investigated the lipid composition of myelin isolated from young adult, middle-aged and aged murine brain. Compact myelin was enriched from cortical regions using a refined density centrifugation protocol and profiled the lipidome using LC-MS/MS. Over 2000 lipid species were identified, with the relative expression of 414 species changing over the age-span. The myelin lipidome from young mice was biochemically distinct compared to the aging cohorts. Aging was characterized by an overall decrease in high abundant species of glycerophospholipid and sphingolipids, including phosphocholine, phosphoethanolamine, sphingomyelin and cerebrosides. Pathway analysis by LipidSig 2.0 and lipid ontology analysis by LION/web highlighted alterations to lipid metabolism and membrane biophysical properties related to bilayer stability and organization. These data support the concept that myelin lipid compositional changes are apparent by middle age which underly structural changes in myelin at later ages. The findings provide greater insight into age-related biochemical alterations in the myelin sheath and may aid in strategies to mitigate white matter degeneration over the lifespan.

Evolutionarily conserved coupling of lysosomal response sensing and disease-associated microglial reprogramming scales with pathological timescales🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-19 · Liang, Y., Yuan, H., Zhao, Y. … Wang, J., Liyi, L., Zhang, Y.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、neurodegeneration、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Background Microglia transition into disease-associated microglia (DAM) during central nervous system (CNS) injury and neurodegeneration, yet the upstream intracellular logic driving this state conversion in vivo remains unclear. The lysosome acts as a signaling hub, but whether physiological phagocytic load universally initiates and quantitatively couples to DAM reprogramming across different pathological timescales is unknown. Methods We established a rigorous, unit-corrected analytical framework to overcome pseudo-replication in public single-cell data, anchoring statistical inferences at the level of independent biological units (animals, sequencing plates, or donors). We integrated six transcriptomic datasets spanning three murine injury/amyloidosis models and three large-scale human neurodegenerative cohorts (>320,000 microglial nuclei), alongside human Alzheimer's disease (AD) GWAS summary statistics. Results We demonstrate that the MiT/TFE-regulated lysosomal biogenesis program temporally precedes DAM reprogramming and maintains an evolutionarily conserved baseline coupling even in uninjured homeostatic tissue. In human microglia, MITF and TFE3 transcripts closely track DAM activation, whereas post-translationally regulated TFEB mRNA does not. Genetic enrichment analysis (MAGMA) reveals that common AD risk does not act upon the upstream lysosomal program, but is strongly anchored downstream at APOE. Furthermore, Trem2-knockout models demonstrate that disease-induced coupling amplification retains roughly half its magnitude even when the mature DAM subpopulation collapses. Cross-system comparisons reveal a state-transition synchrony model: disease-driven amplification of lysosomal-DAM coupling ({Delta}) scales with pathological timescales, showing strong amplification in acute murine injury and aggressive amyloidosis (5xFAD {Delta} = +0.252), but manifesting primarily as steady baseline maintenance across decades-long asynchronous human AD progression (SEA-AD {Delta} = +0.072). Conclusions Our study establishes the lysosomal stress-DAM coupling as an evolutionarily conserved pacemaking module for microglial state transitions, providing a quantitative framework for therapeutically targeting neuroinflammation and tissue repair.

Transcriptomic and Lipidomic Profiling of Human iPSC-Derived Microglia Reveals TREM2-Dependent and -Independent Processes🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Cardona, C. L., Patel, R., Finan, G. M., Kim, T.-W., Sproul, A. A., Teich, A. F.
关键词『amyloid』(命中abstract)
TREM2 has been implicated in AD as a critical regulator of microglial functions and loss of function mutations in TREM2 increases one's risk of developing AD. Given the diverse functional roles of TREM2, we sought to unravel how it affects downstream pathways using human iPSC-derived microglia-like cells (iMGLs) derived from TREM2 knockout (KO) and isogenic control lines and assessing the response to oligomeric amyloid-{beta} 42 (oA{beta}42). While previous studies have primarily used fibrillar A{beta}, we focused on microglial responses to oA{beta}42 given their disease relevance and potent cytotoxic effects. At baseline, we identified a set of TREM2-dependent genes, which corresponded to the DAM signature in mice and disease-associated signatures in humans. Additionally, loss of TREM2 resulted in increased expression of cholesterol biosynthesis genes. In response to oA{beta}42, we found that the transcriptional response was largely TREM2-independent and corresponded to a strong induction of inflammatory pathways, with TREM2 also being downregulated in control iMGLs. We identified subsets of genes and pathways that were unique to either control, such as mitochondrial DNA and RNA degradation, or TREM2 KO iMGLs, such as the senescence-associated secretory pathway and adipogenesis, suggesting some differences in gene expression driven by TREM2 in response to oA{beta}42. Given that the inflammatory response was TREM2-independent, we sought to identify signaling pathways that contributed to inflammation. Inhibition of toll-like receptor signaling significantly reduced the expression of pro-inflammatory genes and modestly increased expression of TREM2 and TREM2-dependent genes. Consistent with our transcriptional data, cytokine production and secretion in response to oA{beta}42 was significantly increased to similar levels in both controls and TREM2 KO iMGLs. Lipidomic profiling of microglia revealed that triglycerides and lactosylceramides were significantly increased in both control and TREM2 KO iMGLs in response to oA{beta}42. TREM2 KO iMGLs had decreased levels of BMPs and increased levels of cholesterol esters across all conditions, further highlighting a role for TREM2 in maintaining lipid homeostasis in microglia. Taken together, we describe genes, pathways, and lipids that are associated with TREM2 expression that likely influence microglial responses to oA{beta}42.

A multimodal arrayed Perturb-seq platform for dissecting microglial state dynamics and target identification🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Ju, L., LaBranche, M. C., Hromadka, S. … Worringer, K. A., Freyre, C., Hsiao, J. S.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Microglia are highly dynamic immune cells of the central nervous system that can adopt diverse functional states in health and disease, making them both emerging therapeutic targets and challenging systems for target identification. Here, we developed TF-iMG, a human pluripotent stem cell-derived transcription factor-induced microglia model generated by inducible expression of SPI1 and CEBPA in Cas9-expressing stem cells. TF-iMG rapidly acquired microglia-like transcriptomic, morphological, inflammatory, and phagocytic features while remaining compatible with efficient lentiviral CRISPR knockout workflows that can be completed within one week. We next established an arrayed multimodal Perturb-seq platform that integrates lentiviral CRISPR knockout, multiplexed single-cell RNA sequencing, cytokine profiling, and live-cell imaging. Single-cell analysis showed that TF-iMG recapitulates intrinsic microglial heterogeneity and captures homeostatic, activated, inflammatory, and proliferative signatures. Stimulation with IL1B and IL4 reproducibly induced distinct pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory states, respectively, providing a framework for perturbation-based interrogation of microglial state regulation. Targeting IL1B and IL4 pathway components validated the platform by attenuating expected stimulus-driven state shifts. Profiling of putative microglial regulators and Alzheimer's disease-associated genes further revealed perturbation-specific transcriptional signatures, altered state occupancy, and multimodal phenotypes spanning cytokine release, morphology, and trajectory. Together, this platform enables scalable target identification in microglia.

A plasma proteomic atlas of rare genetic neurodevelopmental disorders reveals convergent molecular signatures, neurodegenerative overlap, and a cross-disorder severity index🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Ramirez, R. N., Aumann, M., Wieczorek, L. … Poon, W., Bichell, T. J. V., Pfalzer, A. C.（共 37 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、Parkinson、amyotrophic lateral sclerosis』(命中abstract)
Rare genetic neurodevelopmental disorders are individually too small for well-powered biomarker studies, and each is typically studied alone. We present a harmonized plasma proteomic resource that treats them together: over 10,000 proteins measured across 27 rare genetic neurodevelopmental disorders and unaffected family members, 26 of them with enough participants for differential abundance analysis, paired with standardized adaptive behaviour phenotypes and released with the full analysis pipeline. Aggregating across disorders makes shared biology visible that single-disorder studies cannot resolve. Genetically distinct disorders converge onto two proteomic clusters separated along a single dominant axis, distinguishing cytoskeletal and vesicle-trafficking proteins from metabolic and hepatic ones; the grouping is independent of gene function and of clinical classification, indicating convergence on shared downstream biology rather than gene-specific pathology. Epilepsy leaves a reproducible plasma signature detectable across disorders and largely independent of genotype, identifying candidate markers of seizure burden that are shared rather than syndrome specific. These proteomes also overlap those of adult neurodegenerative disease: most disorders are enriched against at least one signature of Alzheimer disease, Parkinson disease, frontotemporal dementia or amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, more often inversely than concordantly, placing early-life monogenic disorders on a shared axis with late-onset degeneration. A multivariate severity index built from plasma proteins tracks adaptive communication ability in disorders held out entirely from model training and distinguishes severity among individuals who share a diagnosis rather than only separating disorders from one another. Twenty-four of the prioritised cross-disorder proteins have a recorded drug interaction, several with agents already approved for other indications. Recruitment was concentrated at single sites for a subset of disorders, and results for those disorders are reported separately and descriptively throughout. Collectively, these findings support a shift from single-gene to pathway-based, cross-disorder trial strategies that leverage convergent biomarker panels and mechanistically informed, potentially repurposed interventions.

Implication of the amyloid precursor protein expressed in GABAergic interneurons for CA3 neuronal dynamics🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Erhardt, A., Moreira de Sa, A., Deforges, S., Ferreira Rodrigues, E., Kees, A. L., Mulle, C.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract/title)
The amyloid precursor protein (APP), a key player in Alzheimers' disease (AD, is ubiquitously expressed in a large majority of neurons, including GABAergic interneurons (INTs) in the hippocampus. INT dysfunction and subsequent abnormalities in neural circuits have emerged as potential mechanisms of cognitive decline in AD. How APP expressed in selective classes of hippocampal INTs controls local neuronal activity and thereby influences brain rhythms remains unclear. We investigated the physiological function of APP in GABAergic INTs with regard to intrinsic excitability, synaptic properties and circuit activity in the hippocampal CA3 region. We selectively deleted APP, and its homolog APLP2 to prevent compensatory mechanisms, in CA3 INTs. We categorized INTs into different clusters based on morphological and electrophysiological properties, and detected only subtle differences in intrinsic excitability and dendritic complexity between control and conditional KO INTs. In contrast, the selective deletion of APP/APLP2 in CA3 INTs impaired the amplitude and kinetics of spontaneous inhibitory currents in CA3 pyramidal cells (PCs). We further performed in vivo silicon probe recordings within CA3 in head-fixed awake mice to investigate the impact of APP/APLP2 deletion on CA3 circuit activity. By analysing oscillations and the spiking activity of putative CA3 PCs and INTs we revealed alterations in the cross-frequency coupling at oscillatory frequencies in the low gamma range, as well as in the strength of connections specifically between INTs. Our work helps better understand the physiological contribution of APP to network activity and to excitatory/inhibitory balance within hippocampal circuits, which are impaired in AD.

Probe-assisted Structural Topography Reveals In-tissue Lesion Heterogeneity in Alzheimer's Disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Wang, F., Sun, X., Zhao, Q. … Liu, C., Piatkevich, K. D., Yang, D.（共 13 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Disease lesions are typically defined by morphology, yet this classification often masks underlying structural heterogeneity. In Alzheimer's disease (AD), amyloid-{beta} (A{beta}) plaques appear as featureless deposits under conventional microscopy, obscuring distinct sub-regions with divergent packing, maturation, and pathological potential. Here, we present Probe-Assisted Structural Topography of Amyloid (PASTA), a single-staining framework that combines a novel pan-A{beta} probe (NapIM) with fluorescence lifetime-resolved detection and super-resolution microscopy to unmask this hidden complexity. Leveraging PASTA, we quantify plaque maturity via the Protofibril Index (PFI) at sub-lesion level, and resolve the three-dimensional (3D) in-tissue architecture of A{beta} plaques at ~30 nm resolution, identifying radially extended A{beta} branches as fundamental structural units. PASTA enables structural mapping across plaque developmental stages to reconstruct their dynamic growth trajectories, resolving two distinct assembly lineages (dense and loose) with divergent growth paths that converge to a mixed terminal subtype and share a common transition point. We further applied PASTA for mapping microglial engagement, linking interdigitated microglial contacts to dense plaque compaction and uncontacted fibril branches to loose morphology. Together, PASTA provides an unprecedented framework for understanding A{beta} heterogeneity, nanoscale architecture, and microglial-mediated remodeling in AD.

PROGMF: Progressive Multimodal Fusion and Representation Learning for Incomplete Neuroimaging Data🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Solomon, V., Calhoun, V. D., Adali, T., Silva, R. F., Angryk, R. A., Liu, J.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Multimodal neuroimaging studies often involve cohorts with partially overlapping data modalities. Many conventional multimodal fusion methods rely on participants with complete observations across the selected modalities. We introduce Progressive Multimodal Fusion (ProgMF), a novel fusion framework for learning multimodal source representations from incomplete neuroimaging data without imputing missing observations. ProgMF integrates modalities sequentially through independent vector analysis (IVA), cross-stage component alignment, and anchor-guided representation transfer, allowing information learned from earlier-stage cohorts to inform subsequent multimodal decompositions. We evaluated ProgMF on four neuroimaging-inspired synthetic modalities, while varying subject overlap, observation noise, modality relatedness, and fusion orders. ProgMF generally achieved lower source-recovery errors than complete-case IVA and the evaluated imputation-based methods, with larger improvements as multimodal subject overlap decreased. We further applied ProgMF to structural MRI gray matter, resting-state functional network connectivity, and 18F-florbetapir (AV45) amyloid positron emission tomography data from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative. ProgMF achieved higher mean cross-modal fusion scores than the imputation-based baselines and a modest improvement over complete-case IVA. Among the 293 participants with complete multimodal observations, two recovered source component vectors showed significant associations with clinical diagnosis across cognitively normal, mild cognitive impairment, and dementia groups. Both sources were also significantly associated with cognitive performance and dementia severity. The recovered sources exhibited diagnosis-related structural and amyloid PET patterns involving regions such as the hippocampi, precuneus, and cingulate cortices. Our findings support progressive representation transfer as an effective approach for recovering coherent, interpretable multimodal sources from participants with incomplete modality coverage.

Molecular analysis of 55 dopamine transporter variants from psychiatric patients reveals convergent mechanisms of dysfunction🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Campana, A., Alves da Silva, L., Degrandmaison, J. … Herborg, F., Stockner, T., Gether, U.（共 14 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Rare genetic variants are increasingly recognized as contributors to neuropsychiatric disease and may reveal underlying pathobiological mechanisms. The dopamine transporter (DAT), encoded by SLC6A3, regulates dopaminergic neurotransmission through rapid dopamine (DA) reuptake. Although disease-associated DAT variants have been linked to disorders ranging from infantile parkinsonism to neuropsychiatric disease, the molecular basis of DAT dysfunction remains poorly understood. Here, we systematically investigated 55 DAT missense variants identified in the iPSYCH2012-nested case-control cohort. By assessing transport activity, inhibitor binding, cell-surface expression, subcellular localization, amphetamine responsiveness and constitutive DA efflux, we resolved the variants into seven phenotypic classes. These reflected distinct molecular defects, including impaired substrate and ion coupling, destabilized intramolecular interactions, and defective folding, trafficking or surface expression. Molecular dynamics simulations provided structural insight into representative variants. Collectively, our findings reveal mechanistic convergence of genetically diverse DAT variants and establish a framework for interpreting the pathogenicity of newly identified SLC6A3 variants.

Alpha-synuclein and neuromelanin converge to promote Parkinsons disease pathology🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Nicolau-Vera, A., Gonzalez-Sepulveda, M., Cladera-Sastre, J. M. … Fu, Y., Halliday, G., Vila, M.（共 16 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、alpha-synuclein』(命中title)
Parkinsons disease (PD) is defined by the preferential degeneration of neuromelanin (NM)-containing dopamine-producing neurons in the substantia nigra (SN), accompanied by intraneuronal inclusions rich in alpha-synuclein (aSYN). However, the mechanistic relationship between NM accumulation and aSYN pathology remains unclear. Here, we reveal a bidirectional pathogenic relationship between aSYN and NM. In human post-mortem midbrain tissue, ventral nigral neurons with high NM content display increased aSYN mRNA expression and are preferentially lost in PD. In a rat model that recapitulates age-dependent, human-like NM accumulation via targeted tyrosinase (TYR) expression, co-expression of aSYN accelerates NM buildup, enhances aSYN oligomerization, and aggravates nigrostriatal degeneration. Mechanistically, aSYN oligomerization in NM-producing rats decreases vesicular dopamine storage and impairs vesicle fusion with the plasma membrane, increasing cytosolic dopamine availability for oxidation into NM. Together, these findings support a pathogenic feedback loop in which elevated aSYN accelerates NM synthesis through disrupted dopamine compartmentalization, while NM accumulation further promotes aSYN aggregation and neuronal loss. This aSYN-NM axis may underlie the preferential vulnerability of pigmented nigral neurons in PD and provide a mechanistic framework for the development of future targeted therapeutics.

The ANKLE2-VRK1 pathway promotes neuronal resilience by preserving proteostasis during aging🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-18 · Dietze, M., Mambou, E. A., Primavera, S., Newlin, H., Link, N.
关键词『Parkinson、tau、neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
ANKLE2 is a key regulator of neural stem cell division through inhibition of the kinase VRK1, and mutations in either ANKLE2 or VRK1 cause rare neurodevelopmental disorders that impact brain growth and development. Recent evidence showing that reduced ANKLE2 activity promotes Tau aggregation in vitro suggests that ANKLE2 may also contribute to age-related neurodegenerative processes; however, its role post-development remains unknown. Using Drosophila, we show a novel role for the Ankle2-ballchen (VRK1) pathway in maintaining neuronal resilience and proteostasis. Pan-neuronal and motor neuron reduction of Ankle2 in adult animals shortens lifespan, impairs motor function and accelerates neuronal loss. Lifelong reduction produces more severe phenotypes including an upright wing posture reminiscent of Drosophila Parkinsons disease models. Mechanistically, loss of Ankle2 causes the accumulation of ubiquitinated proteins throughout the central nervous system, indicating impaired protein clearance. Ankle2 mutants have reduced proteasome activity with activation of compensatory degradative pathways, supporting this hypothesis. Loss of Ankle2 behavioral and protein accumulation phenotypes are rescued by expression of human ANKLE2, indicating neuronal ANKLE2 function is conserved between flies and humans. Importantly, VRK1/Ballchen reduction also rescues ubiquitin accumulation and behavior phenotypes caused by loss of Ankle2, demonstrating that the developmental Ankle2-VRK1 pathway is preserved and remains functionally important in aged neurons. More broadly, our findings reveal that a conserved developmental ANKLE2- VRK1 pathway is repurposed in aged neurons to maintain proteostasis and neuronal resilience, providing a mechanistic framework linking neurodevelopmental disease genes to age-associated neurodegeneration.
