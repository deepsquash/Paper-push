Dual kinase inhibition provides early clues to restoration of neuroimmune homeostasis in an AAV-haSyn model of Parkinsons disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-07 · Chiang, W., Sandoval, I. M., Li, H. … Wagner, E. J., Manfredsson, F. P., Gelbard, H. A.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Background Previous studies of drugs targeting mixed linage kinase type 3 (MLK3) and/or leucine rich repeat kinase type 2 (LRRK2) in sporadic Parkinsons disease (PD) have failed to meet clinical endpoints despite multiple lines of evidence implicating these kinases in pathogenesis. This has reduced enthusiasm for MLK3 and LRRK2 as druggable targets. Largely overlooked is the evidence that these targets remain valid but must be considered in combination to achieve a therapeutic outcome. This provides the rationale for this study. Methods We conducted a pilot study modeling early, pre-symptomatic PD with subsequent evaluation of therapeutic potential of a multi-kinase inhibitor (URMC-099) in alleviating disease pathology. Sprague Dawley rats received unilateral intranigral injections of low dose AAV-haSyn. After a two-week recovery period, all animals were administered either once-daily intraperitoneal (i.p.) injection of URMC-099 or its vehicle control until their terminal endpoint. Induction of early PD, and its alleviation with URMC-099, was evaluated by lateralized motor impairment using the cylinder test and immunohistochemical analysis of neuropathology in the substantia nigra (SN) and striatum. Finally, an in vitro microglial PD model exposed to aSyn pre-formed fibrils and treated with URMC-099 was used to further evaluate the mechanism underlying therapeutic activity. Results A unilateral model of early and progressive AAV-haSyn model for PD was used to demonstrate the therapeutic potential of a targeted multi-kinase inhibitor to attenuate PD-associated neuropathology and prevent the manifestation of lateral motor deficits. Immunohistochemical analysis revealed protection of neuronal cellular integrity along the nigral-striatal pathway with once-daily i.p. URMC-099. The mitigation of degenerative neuropathology was linked to targeted inhibition of MLK3 and LRRK2 and complemented by reduced levels of gliosis in astrocytes and microglia in the SN. Single-microglia segmentation analysis suggested URMC-099 treatment preserved dynamic effector state switching in microglia to enhance autophagic flux to reduce the overall burden of pathologic aSyn. Transcriptomic analysis of a complementary in vitro microglia model corroborated the retention of a homeostatic-like effector state with URMC-099 treatment that was unique to MLK3/LRRK2 dual inhibition. Conclusions Unilateral injection of low dose AAV-haSyn successfully resulted in a lateralized model of early, progressive PD. Dual inhibition of aberrant MLK3 and LRRK2 activity with once-daily i.p. URMC-099 therapy significantly mitigated the development of PD pathology and progression in an early manifest phenotype. Our results present a potential mechanism of therapeutic action dependent on the dual inhibition of MLK3 and LRRK2. This presents a novel therapeutic modality that may be beneficial to a broad population of etiologic PD patients.

Personalized Prediction of Differences in fMRI Connectivity Estimates Across Preprocessing Pipelines🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Maximov, Y., Volkov, A., Chattopadhyay, T., Thompson, P. M., Gabitov, I.
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Longitudinal functional MRI of the human brain is valuable for tracking changes in functional connectivity (FC) associated with disease progression or treatment response. Even so, measured FC values depend on preprocessing, and nuisance regression and temporal filtering affect individuals differently. We examined whether we could use earlier scans to predict how preprocessing would affect a later scan. In 69 ADNI (Alzheimers Disease Neuroimaging Initiative) participants scanned three times, we measured how nuisance regression and filtering, alone and in sequence, changed the measured FC. A ridge regression model combined these responses with baseline FC from the third scan to predict the effect of reversing the processing order. Just four coefficients were shared across 79,800 connections. In leave-one-participant-out testing, mean squared error was 0.012, compared with 0.017 for predicting the same average response for everyone. It outperformed the best of seven alternative predictions chosen for each participant (0.013; normalized gain 0.069, 95% bootstrap interval 0.006-0.134), with lower error in 44 of 69 participants. About half the benefit of using each persons earlier responses, rather than the average across participants, came from which connections changed. This advantage persisted after accounting for acquisition variables. The ADNI-trained model reduced mean error in ten Midnight Scan Club participants without refitting. These results support using personal preprocessing history to predict changes in connectivity estimates on newly acquired scans.

Dopamine synthesis capacity and tau pathology differentially modulate rest-to-task functional network dynamics🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Morin, T. M., Chen, H.-Y., Aghasoltan, S., Hsu, M. M., Jagust, W. J., Berry, A. S.
关键词『Alzheimer、tau、amyloid』(命中abstract/title)
Optimal cognition relies on large-scale functional brain networks which balance segregation at rest with selective integration during task performance. In aging, these network dynamics are altered. We examined two prominent features of brain aging: dysregulated dopamine function and Alzheimer's disease (AD)-related pathology to examine impacts on brain network dynamics. Young adults and cognitively unimpaired older adults completed resting-state and task-based fMRI and PET imaging of dopamine synthesis capacity ([18F]fluoro-m-L-tyrosine). Older adults were classified as {beta}-amyloid negative/positive using [11C]Pittsburgh Compound-B PET(PiB- or PiB+) and underwent tau-PET scanning ([18F]flortaucipir). Greater rest-to-task network stability and task-related network segregation were associated with better subsequent memory. Compared to young adults, PiB+ older adults showed reduced network stability and both PiB+ and PiB- older adults showed reduced segregation during rest. Higher ventral striatal dopamine synthesis capacity was associated with greater stability within associative networks in young adults, but this relationship was attenuated in older adults and absent in PiB+ participants. In PiB+ older adults, higher tau burden was associated with a task-induced collapse of network segregation across large-scale networks. These findings reveal dissociable influences of dopamine and tau on rest-to-task network organization, highlighting the value of integrating neuromodulatory and pathological markers in aging research.

Network analysis of mouse brain proteomes resolves molecular and cellular endophenotypes of aging and Aβ pathology🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Rayaprolu, S., Malepati, S., Dammer, E. B. … Liang, S., Seyfried, N. T., Rangaraju, S.（共 18 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Aging and Alzheimer's disease (AD) pathology exert both shared and distinct effects on the brain, yet their relative contributions to molecular alterations remain poorly defined. Here we performed tandem mass tag proteomics across the lifespan in wild-type and 5xFAD mice and integrated these data with human brain proteomic networks to resolve aging- and amyloid-beta (A{beta}-associated molecular programs. We quantified 8,535 proteins and identified 51 co-expression modules representing neuronal, mitochondrial, glial, vascular and proteostatic pathways. Aging emerged as the predominant driver of proteomic remodeling, with widespread alterations in RNA metabolism, protein translation, and neuronal pathways occurring independently of A{beta} pathology. In contrast, A{beta} selectively modified a subset of age-dependent processes, including suppression of mitochondrial ATP biosynthetic machinery and amplification of astrocytic and microglial activation states. These key aging and disease-related molecular signatures were highly conserved in human AD brain proteomes. Together, our findings establish aging as the primary determinant of brain proteome organization while revealing specific mechanisms through which A{beta} pathology reshapes age-associated biological processes. Our results also highlight molecular mechanisms of human aging and AD pathology that can be modeled in mouse models.

Broad Cognitive and Motivational Enhancement through M4-Selective Muscarinic Modulation🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Wen, X., Shereen, S., Neumann, A. … Lindsley, C. W., Jones, C. K., Womelsdorf, T.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
The muscarinic M4 subtype of acetylcholine receptors is part of agonist compounds considered treatment options for Schizophrenia and Alzheimers disease, but how M4-subtype selective modulation affects cognition and motivation is largely unknown due to dose-limiting side effects, a scarcity of receptor-selective compounds, and missing multi-domain assessments of cognition. Here, we surmount these constraints by utilizing a M4-selective positive allosteric modulator (PAM) to assess in nonhuman primates a broad array of endpoints spanning motivational effort control and multiple executive function domains. We found broad dose-dependent performance improvements of set shifting indexing cognitive flexibility, visual search indexing selective attention, antisaccade performance indexing inhibitory control, visuospatial maze learning, and temporal order serial object learning, while working memory span was non-systematically modulated. In addition, the M4 PAM reduced effort avoidance in an effort-based choice task documenting a pro-motivational effect. Task-composite performance scores showed pro-cognitive effects across multiple doses and an effective dose range for individual subjects in the absence of adverse dose-limiting cholinergic side effects. Together, the broad pro-cognitive and pro-motivational multi-task profile of M4 PAM modulation suggests that the M4 receptor is a master regulator of executive cognitive functioning, possibly acting on domain-general neural processes across the prefrontal-striatal circuits.

Phase-locked dual-band vibration bursts strengthen anti-kindling by vibrotactile coordinated reset stimulation in a spiking network model🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Patki, S. A., SKM, V.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Parkinsons disease and related movement disorders are accompanied by pathologically synchronized neuronal activity in cortico-basal ganglia circuits. Coordinated reset (CR) stimulation was derived computationally as a means of suppressing such activity at its source, by resetting the phases of the subpopulations of a synchronized network at staggered times. Under spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP), the resulting decorrelated firing depresses excitatory coupling and establishes a weakly coupled, desynchronized state that outlasts stimulation, a process termed anti-kindling. Sustained post-stimulation benefit has been demonstrated for electrical CR stimulation in parkinsonian primates and in proof-of-concept patient studies, and vibrotactile CR (vCR) stimulation realizes the same principle non-invasively through bursts of fingertip vibration. The deployed vCR protocol employs a carrier frequency of 250 Hz, which preferentially recruits Pacinian afferents. Because Pacinian receptive fields extend substantially beyond the stimulated digit, inputs originating at adjacent fingertips converge on overlapping regions of the target population, which degrades the spatial separation on which CR depends. Here we examine whether the spectral composition of the individual burst, independent of the stimulation schedule, governs the magnitude of anti-kindling. In a Hodgkin-Huxley network with STDP, driven through a two-channel model of fast-adapting afferents, a dual-band burst superimposing a 30 Hz component on the 250 Hz carrier yielded the lowest post-stimulation coupling among eleven vCR patterns, at one third of the carriers drive power. Recruitment of focal Meissner afferents by the 30 Hz component restricts part of the drive to the stimulated subpopulation, and phase locking of that component to the burst envelope generates post-burst pauses that reset the phase of the stimulated neurons. Spectral composition therefore determines both the spatial selectivity of vCR stimulation and its capacity for phase resetting.

Seed Amplification Assay-Based Detection of Alpha-Synuclein Aggregates Outperforms Immunohistochemical Analysis in the Intestinal Mucosal Biopsies of Parkinson's Disease and Multiple System Atrophy Participants🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Keshavarzian, A., Engen, P. A., Soto, C. … Abdel-Mohsen, M., Sharma, A., Goetz, C. G.（共 14 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson、alpha-synuclein』(命中title)
Objective: To determine whether an -synuclein (-Syn) seed amplification assay (SAA) detects seeding-competent aggregates in endoscopic mucosal biopsies, differs by gastrointestinal region, registers pathology more frequently than immunohistochemistry (IHC), and associates with Parkinson disease (PD) diagnosis, disease duration or severity. Design: This cross-sectional, proof-of-concept diagnostic accuracy study analyzed duodenum, ileum, and sigmoid biopsies from PD participants, age- and sex-matched healthy controls (HC), plus exploratory multiple system atrophy (MSA) subtypes [parkinsonian (MSA-P) and cerebellar (MSA-C)]. Ileal and sigmoid PD and HC biopsies were assessed using proteinase K-resistant phospho-Ser129 IHC. SAA positivity was evaluated against clinical and biological measures. Results: In PD, SAA detected seeding competent -Syn aggregates with high observed specificity (97.5-100%) and region-dependent sensitivity (18/37, 48.65% in ileum and 36/56, 64.29% in sigmoid). Sigmoid SAA positivity increased significantly with PD disease duration (40.00% at <5 years, 73.33% at 5-10 years, and 93.75% at >10 years), independent of motor severity, whereas ileal positivity remained stable across disease stages. In 37 paired sigmoid biopsies, SAA detected -Syn aggregates 2.8-fold more frequently than IHC. Sigmoid seeding activity was detected in all five MSA-P, but not in two MSA-C. IHC did not detect aggregates in any MSA. Conclusions: In PD, -Syn SAA detected seeding-competent -Syn aggregates in endoscopic mucosal biopsies with high observed specificity, moderate sensitivity, and approximately 2.8-fold higher detection rate than IHC in sigmoid. Positive SAA in MSA patients supports GI involvement across synucleinopathies. SAA detection in PD differed by intestinal region and disease duration, supporting further validation of intestinal SAA as a PD biomarker.

The G-protein coupled receptor GPR34 maintains the homeostatic state of microglia and restrains the disease-associated microglial response in an Alzheimers disease model🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-06 · Geller, A., Kwon, M. J., Simmons, S. K. … Zhang, Y.-L., Sheng, M., Kunwar, P. S.（共 35 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中title/abstract)
Expression of the G protein coupled receptor GPR34 is highly enriched in microglia and is downregulated in various brain diseases, including Alzheimers disease (AD) and multiple sclerosis (MS). GPR34 function is poorly understood, as is its role in regulation of microglial states. Using RNA-sequencing, we find that microglia from Gpr34 knockout (KO) mouse brains exhibited a transcriptomic shift toward disease-associated microglia (DAM) and inflammatory profiles, partially resembling the microglial phenotype seen in 5xFAD AD model mice. Moreover, when Gpr34 KO mice were crossed with 5xFAD mice, the DAM transcriptional profile of microglia and glial pathology were further exacerbated beyond the already robust DAM signature driven by 5xFAD alone. This occurred without affecting amyloid plaque burden. Human stem cell-derived microglia (iMGLs) lacking GPR34 showed reduced calcium (Ca2+) and phosphorylated ERK (pERK) signaling in response to stimulation with known GPR34 agonists (lyso-phosphatidylserine (lysoPS) and myelin), as well as transcriptomic changes in immune regulation and cell proliferation related pathways. Interestingly, GPR34 KO iMGLs were selectively impaired in phagocytosis of myelin but not amyloid-B; (AB) or E. coli, and showed a diminished transcriptional response elicited by myelin. Together, these findings suggest that GPR34 is important for maintaining microglia in a homeostatic state, promotes phagocytosis of and transcriptional response to myelin, and limits microglial reactivity in neurodegenerative disease conditions.
