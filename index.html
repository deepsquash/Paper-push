The TRIM32 granule promotes TDP-43 inclusion formation and amyloid aggregation through shuttle condensates🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Meng, F., He, Z., Zhou, J. … Troncoso, J. C., Worley, P. F., Zhang, W.（共 8 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、amyotrophic lateral sclerosis、amyloid』(命中abstract/title)
Aberrant protein aggregation and inclusion formation are hallmarks of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and frontotemporal lobar degeneration (FTLD), which share overlapping genetic and pathological features. Similar aggregates are increasingly recognized in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and limbic-predominant age-related TDP-43 encephalopathy (LATE). However, it remains unclear whether a shared molecular pathway drives this pathological aggregation. Here, we report the formation of stress-responsive cytoplasmic TRIM32 granules, distinct from stress granules. The E3 ubiquitin ligase TRIM32, together with the shuttle factor UBQLN2 and the autophagy adaptor p62/SQSTM1, drive condensation of TRIM32 granules that depend on E3 ligase activity and a network of intermolecular interactions. These condensates act as scaffolds that capture client proteins, including TDP-43 and ANXA11, and modulate their mobility. A unique hydrophobic loop within TRIM32's substrate-binding domain mimics low-complexity motifs in ANXA11 and TDP-43, enabling selective retention via competitive binding mediated by the UBQLN2 STI1 domain. Moreover, TRIM32 granules promote amyloid aggregation of TDP-43, an effect that is exacerbated by a pathogenic UBQLN2 mutation. In brains from individuals with diverse neurodegenerative diseases, TRIM32 co-localizes with pathological phospho-TDP-43 (pTDP-43) inclusions, supporting a model that TRIM32-driven granules function as selective proteostasis sorting compartments that broadly contribute to TDP-43 proteinopathy.

Sleep promotes neuronal autophagy for intracellular clearance🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Yang, L., Zhang, X., Li, J., Liu, X., Liu, D.
关键词『amyloid』(命中abstract)
Sleep is essential for brain health, yet how it maintains neuronal homeostasis at the cellular level remains poorly understood. Here, we show that extended wakefulness selectively suppressed neuronal autophagic flux in the cortex of mice, whereas sleep rapidly restored it, accompanied by a shift in autophagic cargo from mitochondrial components to synaptic proteins. Sleep upregulated ATG7 expression in the cortex, involving both lysosomal degradation and a putative noncanonical secretory route. Moreover, low neuronal activity is associated with efficient flux, as Gq-mediated neuronal activation impaired autophagy and promoted intracellular amyloid-beta; accumulation. Notably, closed-loop optogenetic stimulation of lysosomal acidification, exclusively during sleep, reduced amyloid pathology in 5xFAD mice, whereas wake-targeted stimulation worsened pathology. Together, these findings demonstrate that sleep serves as a physiological gatekeeper for cortical neuronal autophagy, and highlight the importance of brain-state timing for enhancing intracellular proteostasis in neurodegenerative disease.

An AAV model of tauopathy targeting the mouse dentate gyrus reveals greater trans-synaptic tau spreading with aggregation-prone tau variants🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Leal, P., Duwat, C., Adamski, C. … Bonvento, G., Cambon, K., Bemelmans, A.-P.（共 16 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract/title)
Alzheimer's disease is characterized by progressive memory and cognitive decline, which is strongly correlated with the anatomical progression of tau pathology. Resulting from the intraneuronal aggregation of misfolded tau protein, tauopathy propagates in an anterograde manner between synaptically connected neurons through a prion-like mechanism. While glial cells, particularly microglia, are implicated in this process, the precise mechanisms underlying tauopathy propagation remain poorly understood. A critical unresolved question is whether aggregated or soluble pathologic tau forms mediate this spread. Here, we show in vivo that tau spreading scales with the aggregation propensity of the tau variant expressed. We developed a mouse model using adeno-associated virus (AAV)-mediated gene transfer to overexpress distinct tau forms in the dentate gyrus, alongside a reporter gene. This design enabled discrimination between transduced neurons and those secondarily affected by pathology propagation. Leveraging contralateral neuronal projections, we quantified propagation levels. To investigate the role of microglia and of extracellular vesicles, we analyzed microglial morphology in response to different tau forms and measured tau protein levels in extracellular vesicles isolated from injected mouse brains. We first confirmed the presence of vector-derived tau protein in non-transduced neurons, validating our model for studying tau propagation. We then found that aggregation-prone tau variants were associated with a greater number of tau-positive neurons in the contralateral hippocampus than wild-type tau. Additionally, extracellular vesicles from brains injected with aggregated tau contained higher levels of tau protein. Given that insoluble tau aggregates represent the ultimate stage of tauopathy, our findings suggest that propagation occurs at advanced stages of tau pathology, and that extracellular vesicles may contribute to the transfer of pathological tau, although the cellular origin of these vesicles was not determined in this study. Furthermore, our model provides a robust experimental paradigm for exploring therapeutic strategies aimed at inhibiting tauopathy propagation.

PKR drives neuropathology in a mouse model of Leigh syndrome🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-12 · Gonzalez-Torres, M., Bianchi, P., Blanco-Ramos, M. … Prigione, A., Sanz, E., Quintana, A.（共 24 位作者）
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Mitochondrial dysfunction is increasingly recognized as a trigger of innate immune responses, yet whether these responses contribute to neurodegeneration remains poorly understood. Here, we uncover a cell-type-specific antiviral program in vulnerable GABAergic neurons of Ndufs4-deficient mice and human brain organoids modeling Leigh syndrome. Despite robust type I interferon responses, genetic ablation of innate immune mediators Ifnar1 or Sting1 fails to ameliorate disease progression. Instead, we identify the double-stranded RNA sensor protein kinase R (PKR) as a critical mediator of mitochondrial neuropathology. PKR associates with mitochondrial RNA, while its genetic deletion or silencing delays neurological decline, attenuates neuroinflammation, and extends survival without restoring mitochondrial respiration. PKR deletion also normalizes JNK signaling without detectable global translational repression, suggesting a noncanonical contribution to disease. Together, our findings reveal PKR-mediated antiviral RNA sensing as a pathogenic mechanism in mitochondrial complex I deficiency and highlight PKR as a candidate target for mitochondrial encephalopathies.

A shared slowing axis and phenotype-specific rhythms link clinical EEG to FDG-PET dementia phenotypes🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-11 · Satyadev, N., Gogineni, V., Corriveau-Lecavalier, N. … Breakspear, M., Lowe, V. J., Jones, D. T.（共 15 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract)
Early and accurate identification of neurodegenerative disorders has notable implications for treatment and quality of life. Whereas neuroimaging-based biomarkers can accurately identify neurodegenerative subtypes, EEG represents a low-cost alternative that could expand access to dementia care. Here, we investigated whether six data-driven EEG latent factors, derived by nonnegative tensor decomposition of clinical EEGs, differ systematically across FDG-PET-defined neurodegenerative phenotypes. In 455 patients with EEG and FDG-PET acquired within one year of each other, factor expression was compared across 14 diagnostic classes assigned by StateViewer, a validated artificial intelligence framework for FDG-PET interpretation. To separate disease-general severity from phenotype-specific topography, each factor was also regressed on a subject-level slowing index. Finally, we related the factors to regional FDG uptake and to neuropsychological performance (n = 98) and assessed their potential utility for single-subject classification. Four factors reflecting neurophysiological oscillations (posterior alpha, Rolandic beta, frontotemporal delta-theta and posterior theta-slow alpha) varied significantly across phenotypes, with attenuation of faster and augmentation of slower rhythms most pronounced in dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), dysexecutive Alzheimer disease (AD) and posterior cortical atrophy, whereas factors dominated by eye-movement and muscle artifact showed no robust phenotypic association. Alpha and beta effects were largely attributable to overall slowing, whereas posterior theta-slow alpha became more phenotype-specific after adjustment (eta-squared 0.082 to 0.147), dissociating the Alzheimer disease spectrum from frontotemporal syndromes. The slowing index tracked with an FDG-PET pattern highly associated with DLB across the cohort, irrespective of assigned phenotype (r = 0.51, P < 0.001). Consistent with a model in which dementia phenotypes reflect altered hippocampal-neocortical oscillatory coupling (stochastic latent oscillatory diffusion), frontotemporal delta-theta tracked a limbocortical axis of metabolic pattern expression, rising with neocortical and falling with limbic-predominant involvement, independent of overall slowing and phenotype. Each factor mapped onto a distinct metabolic topography, with frontotemporal delta-theta resembling Alzheimer-type hypometabolism and posterior theta-slow alpha the medial occipital hypometabolism of DLB. In terms of neuropsychological performance, Rolandic beta was associated with executive functions, and frontotemporal delta-theta with memory, visuospatial and global performance. Using these four factors alone, an exploratory logistic regression classifier separated cognitively unimpaired participants from DLB, posterior cortical atrophy, typical AD, and dysexecutive AD (area under the curve > 0.8) but poorly discriminated frontotemporal dementia phenotypes canonically associated with 4-repeat tau. Thus, data-driven decomposition of clinical EEG separates oscillatory activity from artifact into factors that track metabolic phenotype, regional metabolism and cognition, capturing both overall slowing and phenotype-specific patterns. This work informs the further development of scalable, biology-informed EEG-based phenotyping of neurodegenerative disease.

Chronic FMNL2 overexpression is associated with changes in brain histology and exploratory behavior in adult zebrafish🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-11 · Lyu, C., Tayran, H., Bhattarai, P., Yilmaz, E., Kizil, C.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Formin-like protein 2 (FMNL2) has been associated with Alzheimer`s disease and cerebrovascular pathology and regulates gliovascular remodeling following amyloid exposure. However, the consequences of sustained FMNL2 elevation remain unclear. We generated a transgenic zebrafish line expressing human FMNL2 under the her4.1 promoter and examined adult brain histology and behavior under baseline conditions and following acute amyloid-{beta}42 (A{beta}42) exposure. Histological analyses assessed FMNL2 immunoreactivity, overlap between the glial marker BLBP and endothelial tight-junction marker ZO1, synaptic vesicle protein SV2 labeling, and microglial counts. Exploratory behavior was evaluated using the novel tank test and free-movement pattern Y-maze. Compared with uninjected wild-type controls, uninjected FMNL2-overexpressing fish showed higher FMNL2 immunoreactivity, lower BLBP-ZO1 overlap, fewer normalized SV2 puncta, and higher counts of total and morphologically activated microglia. Acute A{beta}42 exposure in wild-type animals produced changes in the same direction across these histological endpoints. No significant additional histological changes were detected following A{beta}42 exposure in the overexpression background. In the novel tank test, uninjected FMNL2-overexpressing fish had a higher composite boldness index than uninjected wild-type controls. These findings indicate that sustained FMNL2 overexpression is associated with histological changes resembling several responses to acute A{beta}42 exposure, together with altered exploratory behavior. They support further investigation of persistent FMNL2 elevation as a contributor to altered glial-vascular organization and cellular responses relevant to Alzheimer`s disease.

Prenatal Alcohol Exposure Disrupts γ-Secretase Activity and Impairs Learning and Memory in Wild-Type and 3xTg-AD Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-11 · Montenegro, P. C., Kim, R., Zedek, M., Chicas, M., Yeh, P. W. L., Yeh, H. H.
关键词『Alzheimer、amyloid』(命中abstract)
Although prenatal alcohol exposure (PAE) has been proposed as an early- life risk factor for Alzheimer's disease and related dementias (AD/ADRD), the mechanistic underpinnings are underexplored. Mutations in the Presenilin genes contribute to AD/ADRD, with Presenilin 1 acting as the catalytic subunit of the gamma-secretase complex responsible for cleaving Notch and amyloid precursor protein (APP). We hypothesized that PAE disrupts gamma-secretase activity during brain development, which persists and is associated with behavioral deficits later in life. Pregnant wild-type B6129 and 3xTg-AD mice were fed an ethanol-containing liquid diet during gestational days 13-15. From birth to adulthood, PAE increased APP C-terminal fragments and Notch intracellular domain (NICD) levels in cortical lysates. These changes were accompanied by impaired hippocampal-dependent learning and memory in wild-type mice at 3 and 6 months of age and exacerbated behavioral deficits in 4-month-old 3xTg-AD mice. Our findings provide the first mechanistic insight linking PAE to AD/ADRD vulnerability.

Toward pathology-guided illumination optimization for neuromodulation with a foundation-model-encoded light transport surrogate🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-11 · Dong, S., Guan, M., Yang, L. … Ren, W., Ni, R., Wei, X.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『amyloid』(命中abstract)
Clinical treatment planning for near-infrared (NIR) light neuromodulation requires patient-specific dosimetry to optimize light fluence (LF) delivery to cortical targets. The gold-standard Monte Carlo photon-transport forward solver is accurate but computationally expensive and non-differentiable for personalized inverse design across subjects. Here, we present PhomiNeuro, a foundation-model-encoded differentiable surrogate for time-resolved LF modeling and pathology-guided inverse design. A pretrained 3D medical imaging foundation model (VISTA3D) was domain-adapted to 285 training head models annotated with optical properties, and then coupled to an implicit neural representation (INR) that predicts LF at arbitrary spatiotemporal coordinates. Regularized by a time-dependent diffusion-equation residual, it demonstrated superior fidelity in 84 held-out participants, and ablations identified VISTA3D-derived anatomical priors as dominant and physics regularization as complementary. PhomiNeuro enables fast and robust queries of LF and its gradients with respect to illumination parameters for targets guided by individual structural magnetic resonance imaging and amyloid positron emission tomography, achieving 466-fold per-iteration speedup and 167-fold end-to-end optimization speedup. Explanatory analyses of LF delivery variability, including sex differences and amyloid status, identified structural variations as the primary drivers. These results position PhomiNeuro as a highly extensible translational framework toward personalized treatment planning and digital twin development in precision neuromodulation.

Neuropathological assessment of cerebrovascular integrity in Chronic Traumatic Encephalopathy🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-11-11 · Martinac, A. E., DeLorenzo, A., Dik, D., Pasas, L., Bilston, L. E., Shepherd, C. E.
关键词『tau』(命中abstract)
Chronic traumatic encephalopathy neuropathological change (CTE-NC) is defined by perivascular deposits of phosphorylated tau, with lesions preferentially located at sulcal depths. We investigated whether CTE-NC is associated with structural changes to perivascular spaces (PVSs) or microvascular degeneration. Post-mortem middle frontal gyrus tissue from 11 CTE-NC cases and 11 age-matched controls was examined using immunohistochemistry. All CTE-NC cases exhibited pathognomonic lesions in this region. We quantified PVS width along with vessel lumen and PVS area, string vessel burden, pericyte density, and aquaporin-4 (AQP4) polarisation to the astrocytic endfoot. Total PVS width did not differ significantly between the groups in grey matter (P = 0.543) nor white matter (P = 0.468). Neither PVS area (sulcal depth, P = 0.097; gyral crest, P = 0.645) nor PVS-to-lumen area ratio (sulcal depth, P = 0.454; gyral crest, P = 0.307) differed significantly. Similarly, there were no significant differences in total microvascular length (p = 0.693), string vessel burden (p = 0.128), pericyte density (p = 0.589), or AQP4 endfoot enrichment (p = 0.538). We therefore found no evidence of large-scale PVS enlargement, lumen narrowing, capillary degeneration, pericyte loss, or altered AQP4 endfoot enrichment in CTE-NC at the locations and spatial scales investigated. Taken together, these findings suggest that the pathognomonic perivascular tau lesion is not associated with a classical small vessel degenerative phenotype in CTE-NC. However, that does not exclude more subtle molecular, functional, or ultrastructural changes at the interface between vessels, the perivascular space, and surrounding brain tissue.
