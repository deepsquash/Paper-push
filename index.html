LRRC8A/D-containing anion channels mediate swelling-activated taurine release in the mouse brain🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-23 · Chandler, M. L., Nalwalk, J. W., Dohare, P., Sprague, A. D., Schober, A. L., Mongin, A. A.
关键词『tau』(命中title)
Volume-regulated anion channels (VRACs) are swelling-activated Cl- channels formed by heteromers of LRRC8 proteins (LRRC8A-E). VRACs mediate regulatory volume decrease by releasing Cl- and organic osmolytes, including taurine. In the brain, taurine functions as both an osmolyte and an inhibitory neuromodulator, acting as an agonist at glycine and GABAA receptors. Here, we used RNAi and genetically modified mice to (i) determine the contribution of LRRC8 subunits to swelling-activated taurine release, and (ii) test whether brain VRACs contribute to systemic osmoregulation. In astrocyte cultures lacking the essential VRAC subunit LRRC8A, swelling-activated [3H]taurine release was reduced by ~90%. Similarly, in vivo brain microdialysis revealed a [&ge;]90% reduction in swelling-activated taurine release in brain-wide LRRC8A knockout mice (bLRRC8A KO). RNAi experiments further confirmed LRRC8D as a key determinant of VRAC-mediated taurine release, with Lrrc8d knockdown reducing swelling activated [3H]taurine efflux by >50%. RNA-seq revealed enrichment of Lrrc8d in the hypothalamus, where astrocytic taurine release is proposed to inhibit magnocellular neurons and suppress arginine vasopressin secretion during hypoosmotic conditions. We therefore tested whether disrupting brain VRACs impairs recovery from systemic hypoosmotic stress. bLRRC8A KO mice and mice with gene trap-mediated LRRC8D depletion were challenged with an intraperitoneal injection of sterile water (7.5% body weight, under anesthesia), and serum osmolality was monitored over time. Unexpectedly, neither model showed impaired recovery from hypoosmotic challenge. Overall, this study identifies LRRC8A/D-containing VRACs as the primary conduit for swelling-activated taurine release in the brain, yet indicates that this pathway is not essential for maintaining systemic fluid-electrolyte homeostasis.

Splanchnic-spinal transmission of gut-initiated α-synucleinopathy🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-23 · Jung, B.-K., Song, M. K., Kim, Y. … Park, H., Kim, Y.-S., Suh, G. S. B.（共 11 位作者）
关键词『Parkinson』(命中abstract)
Gut-initiated -synucleinopathy is proposed to spread from the gastrointestinal tract to the CNS, contributing to Parkinson's disease (PD), but whether routes beyond the vagus nerve are involved remains unclear. Using complementary intracolonic rotenone and gastroduodenal -synuclein PFF models, we combined pathway-specific surgical interruption with longitudinal behavioral analysis and anatomical mapping of pathological -synuclein. In the intracolonic rotenone model, interruption of the prevertebral ganglia/splanchnic-spinal axis more strongly attenuated behavioral deficits and -synuclein pathology than vagotomy. In the gastroduodenal PFF model, both vagal and splanchnic-spinal pathways contributed to pathology propagation. Early after PFF inoculation, pathological -synuclein was detected in peripheral neural structures associated with both routes. Consistent with these findings, spinal pathology was evident in human PD. Together, these results identify the splanchnic-spinal pathway as a key component of body-first Parkinson's disease, broadening current models of peripheral-to-central disease progression and informing efforts to target early peripheral disease progression.

A coordinated and reproducible multicellular response to AD pathology🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-23 · Vilkaite, G., Volcov, E., An, L. … Mattsson-Carlgren, N., Gaiteri, C., Vogel, J. W.（共 10 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
While single-cell measurements of the Alzheimer's disease (AD) brain allow investigation of vulnerable cells in unprecedented depth, reported findings are heterogeneous, mostly descriptive and often ignore interdependence of cellular responses. Operationalization of these findings requires a multi-cellular model of AD that is robust and reproducible. Here, we utilize a coordinated multicellular community approach across 2.3 million nuclei from 427 ROSMAP donors to identify robust multicellular axes associated with AD pathology and sex. The AD signature comprised profound alteration to RORB-GABRG excitatory neurons and disrupted oligodendrocyte precursor cell signaling. A separate set of AD-related cell-type specific molecular processes with strong sexual dimorphism also emerged. Importantly, these signatures developed and ROSMAP predicted neuropathology and sex, respectively, in a completely independent dataset (SEA-AD). These findings altogether summarize a highly consistent multi-cellular response to AD pathology in the human brain, creating a baseline cellular model to which perturbation and treatment experiments can be compared.

Differential value processing in the rostral and causal subthalamic nucleus during flexible and habitual decision-making in primates: Implications for cognitive dysfunction in Parkinson's disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-23 · Lee, K., Lee, M., Jiang, H., Kim, H. F.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
The subthalamic nucleus (STN) is a major target region of deep brain stimulation (DBS) for Parkinson's disease (PD), effectively alleviating motor symptoms. However, STN-DBS can also induce cognitive impairments, suggesting that the STN contributes to cognitive functions beyond simple motor control. Furthermore, anatomical organization supports functional segregation of cognitive processes, as basal ganglia subregions encoding flexible and stable values topographically project to distinct STN subregions. Yet, whether the STN contributes to cognitive functions and, if so, how subregions of the STN differentially process cognitive flexibility and habitual stability remains unknown. Here, we reveal a rostral-caudal functional organization of the STN that differentially supports flexible value updating and long-term value memory, enabling adaptive and habitual decision-making, respectively. Neurons in the rostral STN (rSTN) preferentially encode flexibly updated values, whereas neurons in the caudal STN (cSTN) preferentially encode stable long-term value memories. Notably, inactivation of the STN along the rostral-caudal axis produced dissociable behavioral deficits: rSTN inactivation selectively impaired goal-directed behavior requiring flexible value updating, whereas cSTN inactivation selectively disrupted habitual behavior guided by long-term value memories. Together, these results reveal a rostral-caudal functional segregation of the STN, with the rSTN supporting goal-directed behavior and the cSTN supporting habitual behavior. These findings further provide a framework for understanding how STN dysfunction or STN-DBS in Parkinson's disease may differentially affect cognitive processes beyond motor control.

Sex-Dependent Effects of Creatine Hydrochloride on Neuroinflammation, Neuronal Survival, and Inhibitory Synaptic Remodeling Following Traumatic Brain Injury in Mice.🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-22 · Zigmond, J. W., Lamberty, B. G., Simon, G. J. … Elliott, N. M., Miller, D. W., Stauch, K. L.（共 7 位作者）
关键词『neurodegeneration』(命中abstract)
Despite traumatic brain injury (TBI) being a leading cause of death and disability in the United States, there is currently no FDA-approved medication that can prevent, heal, or reverse brain damage caused by TBI. TBI is caused by primary and secondary brain damage resulting in loss of neurons, astrocytic gliosis, and microglial activation which culminate in complex neurological disorders. The goal of the current study was to investigate the neuroprotective potential for Creatine Hydrochloride (C-HCl) supplementation following TBI in wild-type mice. Four C-HCl supplementation paradigms were examined including both 7 day pre-injury and 14-day post-injury treatments consisting of the following: 1) water/water, 2) water/C-HCl (100 mg/kg), 3) C-HCl (100 mg/kg)/C-HCl (100 mg/kg), 4) C-HCl (100 mg/kg)/C-HCl (450 mg/kg). Following controlled cortical impact (CCI) injury at a parietal location, measures of tissue and cellular (neuronal and glial) damage were assessed at 14 days post-injury (dpi). At 14 dpi, there were no significant effects on gross anatomical cavitation, tissue loss, third ventricle expansion, or systemic fluid biomarkers with any treatment group, though female mice exhibited larger baseline lesion boundaries overall compared to male mice. However, microstructural evaluation revealed a profound and sex-dependent effect of C-HCl supplementation in the hippocampus with female mice showing reduced neuroinflammation and male mice displaying reduced neurodegeneration (Fluoro-Jade C staining of neurons) across the CA1 and dentate gyrus (DG) compared to placebo control. Furthermore, mice receiving C-HCl pre-injury followed by high-dose C-HCl post-injury demonstrated distinct remodeling of inhibitory circuits to restore network balance with males significantly modifying presynaptic machinery via vesicular GABA transport (VGAT puncta) in the DG, whereas females reinforced postsynaptic architectural framing through scaffolding elements (gephyrin puncta) in both the CA1 and DG. These results suggest C-HCl supplementation holds promise as a neuroprotective therapeutic strategy for TBI. Crucially, the molecular mechanisms utilized to achieve this brain cell homeostasis are radically distinct between the sexes, females benefit primarily via upstream glial stabilization, while males derive protection via direct downstream neuronal survival. These outcomes highlight a critical requirement for sex-stratified profiling in translational pipelines for neurometabolic interventions for TBI. Ultimately, these data indicate that C-HCl successfully counteracts chronic secondary injury cascades, mitigating neurodegeneration, dampening neuroinflammation, and remodeling inhibitory circuitry to prevent post-traumatic network hyperexcitability.

Neuronal expression of E2F4DN restores adult neurogenesis in homozygous 5xFAD mice via TrkB signaling🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-22 · Lozano-Urena, A., Llabres-Mas, A. M., Ramon-Landreau, M. … Cano-Daganzo, V., Valdes-Lora, L., Frade, J. M.（共 9 位作者）
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中abstract)
Background: Alzheimer's disease (AD) is increasingly recognized as a disorder that disrupts brain plasticity long before extensive neuronal loss occurs, including early impairment of adult neurogenesis. However, the molecular mechanisms underlying these alterations remain poorly understood. Methods: Neuronal expression of E2F4 carrying the Thr249Ala/Thr251Ala mutation (E2F4DN) was achieved using an AAV.PHP.eB vector in the 5xFAD mouse model of AD. Neurogenic potential was assessed by immunohistochemistry and quantitative PCR (qPCR) targeting neurogenesis-associated markers. For in vitro experiments, neural stem cells (NSCs) isolated from the subventricular zone (SVZ) were exposed to conditioned media from primary cortical neurons expressing E2F4DN, with or without pharmacological inhibitors. NSC proliferation and differentiation were evaluated by immunocytochemistry and qPCR. Results: Neuronal expression of E2F4DN restored the proliferative and differentiation capacity of NSCs in 5xFAD mice both in vivo and in vitro. Pharmacological inhibition of TrkB signaling abolished the pro-neurogenic effects of E2F4DN-conditioned medium, whereas direct activation of TrkB in NSCs recapitulated these effects. Conclusions: These findings identify a mechanism by which neuronal dysfunction in AD compromises neurogenesis through impaired TrkB-mediated signaling. The results uncover a previously underappreciated paracrine communication between neurons and NSCs and highlight neuronal E2F4DN expression and TrkB modulation as promising therapeutic strategies to counteract early neurogenic deficits in AD.

Early Insulin Signaling Defects and Oxidative Stress Kinase Activation Precede Tau Pathology in PS19 Tau Transgenic Mice🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-22 · Elahi, M., Burd, I., Yang, P., Shen, W.-B.
关键词『Alzheimer、tau』(命中abstract/title)
Pathological tau accumulation is a hallmark of Alzheimers disease (AD), yet its relationship with impaired insulin signaling remains unclear. Previously, we reported altered IRS1 phosphorylation reduced p IR beta, and GLUT3 expression in young AD transgenic mice prior to overt pathology. Here, we investigated insulin signaling and oxidative stress pathways in the Tau P301S (PS19) mouse model. We found that alterations in phospho IRS1, phospho IR beta, and the glucose transporters GLUT3 and GLUT4 occur before pathogenic tau deposition. In stable tau expressing human neuronal cell lines, tau expression was similarly associated with impaired insulin signaling. Concurrent dysregulation of insulin signaling and activation of oxidative stress responsive kinases, accompanied by increased microglial abundance, were evident in 2 month old PS19 mice and persisted into adulthood. Together, these findings demonstrate that insulin signaling abnormalities and oxidative stress kinase activation are early and sustained features of PS19 tauopathy, preceding the onset of overt neuropathology.

Dopaminergic Medication Improves Force Control and Modifies Motor Unit Activity During the Steadiest and Least Steady Periods in Parkinsons Disease🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-22 · Alaei, P., Holobar, A., Wile, D. J., Jakobi, J. M.
关键词『Parkinson』(命中title)
Parkinsons disease (PD) impairs steady force production, but how dopaminergic medication alters force control and the motor unit (MU) activity is unclear. This study examined force steadiness and biceps brachii MU behavior in 16 people with PD ON and OFF dopaminergic medication. Participants performed sustained isometric elbow flexion contractions at 5%, 10%, 15%, and 25% of maximum voluntary contraction (MVC). High density surface electromyography was decomposed into MU spike trains. Force steadiness and MU discharge properties were quantified during nonoverlapping 7-s steadiest and least steady contraction periods, whereas MU coherence was assessed across the force plateau. In the ON state, force steadiness improved by 17_19% during both contraction periods at 5_15% MVC. MU interspike interval variability decreased by [~]12% across all forces during the least steady contraction period and by 17% at 15% MVC during the steadiest contraction period, whereas mean MU discharge rate was unchanged. Recruitment thresholds were higher at 10% and 25% MVC in the ON state, whereas derecruitment thresholds did not differ. Across the entire plateau, alpha- and tremor-band MU coherence decreased by 14% and 18%, respectively, whereas delta-band coherence was unchanged. These findings demonstrate that dopaminergic medication improves both the steadiest and least steady periods of low-force control, accompanied by reduced MU discharge variability and frequency-specific changes in plateau-wide MU coherence rather than changes in mean discharge rate. Resolving within-contraction variation reveals medication-sensitive features of force control that are obscured by conventional plateau-wide analyses, providing a new approach for evaluating motor output in PD.

Frequency-Resolved Connectivity Graphs for EEG-Based Classification of Alzheimer and Frontotemporal Dementia🟢 提前在线NEW 本次新增
bioRxiv · Neuroscience · 在线发布 2026-10-22 · Radwan, M., yazidi, A., lind, P. G.
关键词『Alzheimer』(命中title)
In this study, we build EEG based graph model that uses frequency-resolved connectivity features, with Coherence, phase locking value (PLV) and phase lag index (PLI) in five frequency bands as multi-dimensional edge features. The model is used to classify Alzheimer disease (AD) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD) against healthy controls (CN). This paper extends our earlier work on FTD with AD vs CN as the main new task, a feature ablation for it, and a joint analysis of both tasks. With leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) validation, the model reaches an accuracy of 81.13\% for AD vs CN and 76.06\% for FTD vs CN, which is competitive with the baselines reported with the dataset with far fewer parameters (159 and 282 thousand versus 170.5 million). Under the random cross-validation setting used for the DICE-net model, our model has a comparable accuracy for FTD and a lower accuracy for AD. Node and edge saliency maps highlight occipital and temporal electrodes, the Alpha band for node features and the Theta band for edge features, which is consistent with earlier EEG studies. In an ablation on the AD task, using all 15 edge features performed better than using a single connectivity measure or edge features averaged over the bands.
